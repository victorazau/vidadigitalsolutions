## Step 1 — Plan Your Sequence Before Opening GoHighLevel

Before touching a single button, map out your sequence on paper (or in a doc). An effective law firm sequence includes:

**Recommended structure (14 days):**

| Day | Channel | Message Goal |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| 0 (immediate) | SMS | Confirmation of receipt + next step |
| 1 | Email | Firm introduction + value resource (guide, FAQ) |
| 3 | SMS | Invitation to schedule a consultation |
| 5 | Email | Success story or client testimonial |
| 7 | SMS | Soft reminder + real urgency (deadline, availability window) |
| 10 | Email | Educational content about the lead's type of case |
| 14 | SMS + Email | Final engagement attempt |

**Planning tips before you build:**
- Define the **trigger** (what starts the sequence): a form submission, an ad lead, a chat inquiry.
- Define the **exit condition**: if the lead books an appointment or responds positively, they should exit the sequence automatically.
- Write different messages by practice area (immigration, criminal, family) — personalization can boost open rates by up to 40%.

## Step 2 — Set Up the Workflow in GoHighLevel

Now let's get into the platform. Log into your GoHighLevel account and follow these steps:

**1. Access the Workflow Builder**
- In the left sidebar, click **Automation**.
- Click **Workflows** in the submenu.
- Click the green **+ Create Workflow** button in the top right corner.
- Select **Start from Scratch** (or use a nurturing template if one is available).

*What you'll see on screen:* A blank canvas with a starting node called "Workflow Trigger" at the top. On the left, a panel shows all available trigger options.

**2. Set Your Trigger**
- Click the "Workflow Trigger" node.
- In the side panel, choose the appropriate trigger:
  - **Form Submitted** → if the lead came from a website form.
  - **Tag Added** → if you use tags to identify leads (e.g., "lead-immigration").
  - **Lead Connector / Facebook Lead Ads** → if leads come from paid ads.
- Set the filter: select the specific form or tag.
- Click **Save Trigger**.

**3. Add the First SMS (Day 0 — Immediate)**
- Refer to the [GoHighLevel Workflow Builder guide](/blog/gohighlevel-workflow-builder-complete-guide-2026) to revisit the interface if needed.
- Click the **"+" icon** below the trigger node.
- Select **Send SMS**.
- In the message editor, write something like:
  > *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, we received your message. A member of our team will be in touch shortly. In the meantime, {{location.name}} is ready to help with your case — schedule here: [link]"*
- The variables `{{contact.first_name}}` and `{{location.name}}` are automatically populated by the system.
- Click **Save Action**.

*What you'll see on screen:* The SMS node appears connected to the trigger by a line. A text message icon in the top corner of the node confirms the action type.

## Step 3 — Add Delays and Emails to Your Sequence

**Setting Up a Wait (delay between messages)**

- Click the **"+"** below the SMS node.
- Select **Wait**.
- Set it to **1 day** (for the Day 1 message).
- Check the **Wait until a specific time** option if you want emails to arrive during business hours (recommended: 9–11 AM, Monday through Friday).
- Click **Save**.

**Adding the Day 1 Email**

- Click the **"+"** below the Wait node.
- Select **Send Email**.
- Fill in the fields:
  - **Subject:** "{{contact.first_name}}, here's how we can help with your case"
  - **From Name:** Your firm's name
  - **Body:** Use the visual editor to build an email with a firm introduction, an empathetic paragraph addressing the lead's situation, and a link to schedule a consultation.
- Click **Save Action**.

**Repeat the pattern** for each day in the sequence:
1. Wait (days to pause)
2. Action (SMS or Email)
3. Wait
4. Next action

*What you'll see on screen:* The workflow grows vertically on the canvas, with color-coded nodes — blue for Wait, green for SMS, purple for Email. You can drag nodes to rearrange them visually.

## Step 4 — Set Up an Automatic Exit Condition

This is the step most beginners skip — and it's what prevents an already-converted lead from continuing to receive prospecting messages.

**Option 1: If/Else with a Tag Check**
- After each key action (e.g., the consultation invite), add an **If/Else** node.
- Condition: *"Contact has tag: 'booked-consultation'"*
- If YES → connect to a **Remove from Workflow** node (or redirect to an onboarding sequence).
- If NO → continue the sequence as normal.

**Option 2: Goal Event**
- At the top of the Workflow, click **Settings** (the gear icon).
- Enable **Stop on Goal**.
- Set the goal event: *"Appointment Booked"* or *"Tag Added: active-client"*.
- GoHighLevel will now automatically remove the contact from the sequence as soon as they book.

*What you'll see on screen:* A blue banner at the top of the canvas confirms the Goal is active, showing the name of the configured event.

## Step 5 — Test, Activate, and Monitor

**Testing Before You Go Live**

- In the top right corner of the canvas, click **Test Workflow**.
- Enter a test contact (your own phone number or email).
- Click **Run Test** — the workflow simulates the sequence in real time.
- Verify that SMS messages were delivered, emails are formatted correctly, and all variables populated properly.

**Activating the Workflow**

- Click the toggle in the top right corner: switch from **Draft** to **Published**.
- Confirm in the pop-up window.
- From this point on, any new contact that triggers the workflow will automatically enter the sequence.

**Monitoring Your Results**

- Go to **Automation → Workflows → [your workflow name] → Analytics**.
- Key metrics to track:
  - **Enrollment Rate:** how many leads entered the sequence.
  - **Completion Rate:** how many made it to the end without exiting.
  - **Goal Conversion Rate:** the percentage that converted (booked, responded positively).
- Review the copy of your lowest-performing messages every 30 days.

**Vida Digital Solutions** recommends running the sequence for at least 60 days before making major adjustments — insufficient data leads to bad decisions.

## Advanced Segmentation: Sequences by Practice Area

A generic workflow converts. Segmented workflows convert significantly more. If your firm handles multiple practice areas, build separate sequences for each:

**How to segment in GoHighLevel:**
- On your intake form, add a dropdown field: *"What type of legal help do you need?"* (options: Immigration, Criminal Defense, Family Law, Employment Law).
- In the Workflow, add an **If/Else Branch** immediately after the trigger to split leads by their answer.
- Each branch routes to a different sub-sequence with relevant content.

**Why this matters:** An immigration lead wants to know about visas, green cards, and processing timelines. A family law lead wants to understand divorce proceedings and custody. Generic messages work — specific messages convert.

| Practice Area | Recommended Day 1 Email Content |
|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Immigration | Document checklist for your visa or green card process |
| Criminal Defense | Know your rights: what to do in the first 48 hours |
| Family Law | How the divorce process works in your state |
| Employment Law | Statute of limitations: what the law says about your claim |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many messages should I include in a law firm nurturing sequence?**
**A:** For most firms, between 5 and 8 touchpoints spread over 14 to 21 days is the sweet spot. Sequences that are too short (1–2 messages) have low conversion rates; sequences that are too long (more than 10 messages) increase unsubscribes. Start with 7 touchpoints and adjust based on open and response rate data after 60 days.

**Q: Can GoHighLevel send WhatsApp messages in nurturing sequences?**
**A:** Yes. GoHighLevel offers WhatsApp Business API integration, which can be added as an action inside the Workflow just like SMS and email. For firms that serve Spanish-speaking or immigrant communities in the US, adding WhatsApp to the sequence can significantly boost response rates, since many of these clients use it as their primary communication channel.

**Q: How do I keep messages from feeling like spam or sounding too automated?**
**A:** Use personalization variables (`{{contact.first_name}}`, references to their case type), write in a conversational tone, and avoid heavy legal jargon. Spacing messages out properly (never two emails on the same day) and including genuinely useful content — not just calls to action — keeps the communication feeling human. Also set the sender name to an actual attorney's name, not just the firm name.

Advanced features like WhatsApp Business API integration and AI-powered responses may require add-ons or higher-tier plans. **Q: How long does it take to set up a complete sequence from scratch?**
