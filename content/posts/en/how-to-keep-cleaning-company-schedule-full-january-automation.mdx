## How to Implement This Now (Before January Arrives)

If you're reading this in November or early December, you still have time. Here's the practical step-by-step:

### Step 1 — Organize Your Contacts
Gather all past client contacts — from your phone, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, wherever they live. Separate out anyone who hasn't booked in the last 60 days.

### Step 2 — Build a Reactivation Sequence
Three messages over 8 days is enough for most cases. The first should be personal and direct. The second offers social proof. The third creates urgency with an incentive.

### Step 3 — Set Up Post-Service Follow-Up
Every new booking should automatically enter a relationship sequence: confirmation 24 hours before, a thank-you after the cleaning, a review request, and an invitation to schedule the next appointment.

### Step 4 — Activate Review Collection
A full January also depends on showing up on Google when new clients search. The more recent reviews you have, the better your chances of ranking ahead of competitors. Automating the review request is one of the highest-return actions you can take for the effort involved.
