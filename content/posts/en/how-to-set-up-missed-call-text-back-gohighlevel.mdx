## Step 1 — Access the Automations Section in GoHighLevel

Log in to your GoHighLevel account. In the left-side menu, click on **"Automations"**. You'll see a screen with all your existing workflows.

**What you'll see on screen:** A list of workflows, a blue **"+ New Workflow"** button in the upper right corner, and filter options by status (active/inactive).

Click **"+ New Workflow"** and, in the window that appears, select **"Start from Scratch"**. Give the workflow a clear name — we suggest something like *"Missed Call — Automatic SMS Response"*. Click **"Create Workflow"**.

## Step 2 — Set Up the "Missed Call" Trigger

Inside the workflow editor, the first thing you'll see is a gray block labeled **"Add New Trigger"**. Click on it.

**What you'll see on screen:** A side panel opens on the right with a search bar and a list of available triggers. Type *"missed"* in the search bar.

Select the **"Missed Call"** trigger. It will appear as the first block in your workflow. You don't need to configure advanced filters at this point — the default trigger already captures all missed calls on the number connected to your account.

> **Important tip:** For this trigger to work, your phone number must be configured in GoHighLevel under **Settings > Phone Numbers**. If you haven't done that yet, take care of it before moving on.

## Step 3 — Add the SMS Send Action

With the trigger created, click the **"+"** button below the trigger block to add an action.

**What you'll see on screen:** The same side panel, now showing action categories: Communication, CRM, Sales, etc.

Under the **"Communication"** category, click **"Send SMS"**. An SMS block will appear connected to the trigger. Click on it to open its settings.

In the **"Message"** field, write your automatic response message. Use dynamic variables to personalize it — GoHighLevel offers:

- `{{contact.first_name}}` — the contact's first name (if already in the CRM)
- `{{location.name}}` — your business name
- `{{missed_call.from}}` — the number that called

**Example of a high-converting message:**

> *"Hey {{contact.first_name}}! I saw you tried to reach us and I'm sorry we missed your call. What can we help you with? We're standing by! 😊"*

If you have a booking link (via GoHighLevel Calendar), add it to the message so the customer can schedule a time without needing to call back.

## Step 4 — Add a Delay and a Second Message (Optional but Recommended)

An effective missed-call text-back workflow doesn't stop at one message. The ideal setup is a simple 2-touch sequence:

1. **Immediate message** (done in Step 3): acknowledges the missed call and opens the conversation.
2. **Second message after 1 hour** (if no response): offers an alternative or reinforces the offer.

To do this, click the **"+"** below the SMS block and add the **"Wait"** action. Set it to **1 hour**.

After the delay, add an **"If/Else"** condition: check whether the contact replied to the SMS. If they **did not reply**, add a second SMS message. If they **did reply**, end the workflow (or hand off to a customer service flow).

**Suggested second message:**

> *"Hi there! Just wanted to make sure you got our last message — we'd really love to help you out. Click here to book a time: [link], or just reply to this text with your question. 🙂"*

This two-touch structure increases the average response rate by about 40% compared to a single automated message.

## Step 5 — Set Up the CRM Tag and Pipeline

A lot of people set up the SMS and stop there. But the real power of GoHighLevel is in automatically logging everything in the CRM.

After the SMS block (and before the delay), add the following actions:

### 5a. Add a Tag to the Contact

Click **"+"**, search for **"Add Tag"**, and create a tag like *"Missed Call"* or *"Active Lead"*. This lets you later filter all the leads that came through this flow.

### 5b. Move to a Pipeline

Click **"+"**, search for **"Add to Pipeline"**. Select your sales pipeline and the appropriate stage — for example, *"New Lead"* or *"First Contact"*.

**What you'll see on screen:** A window with a dropdown of available pipelines in your account. Select the pipeline and stage. If you don't have a pipeline set up yet, learn how to create one at [How to Create a Sales Pipeline in GoHighLevel](/blog/how-to-create-sales-pipeline-gohighlevel).

With this step, every missed call automatically becomes a tracked opportunity in your CRM — with the date, phone number, message history, and funnel stage all recorded.

## Step 6 — Activate the Workflow and Test It

With everything configured, click the **"Save"** button in the upper right corner. Then, at the top of the page, toggle the **"Published"** switch (or **"Active"**, depending on your version).

**What you'll see on screen:** The toggle switches from gray to green. This means the workflow is live and ready to fire.

**How to test it:**

1. Use a different cell phone and call the phone number connected to your GoHighLevel account.
2. Let it ring without answering (or reject the call).
3. Wait up to 2 minutes.
4. Check whether the phone you called from received the automatic SMS.
5. In GoHighLevel, go to **Conversations** and confirm that the conversation appeared.

If the SMS didn't arrive, check: (a) whether the number is active under Settings > Phone Numbers, (b) whether the workflow is published, and (c) whether any trigger filters are blocking it from firing.

> **Pro tip from Vida Digital Solutions:** Always test with a real phone number before considering the setup complete. SMS delivery issues are more common than they seem and are usually resolved by checking your A2P 10DLC registration settings (required for commercial SMS sending in the US).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does missed-call text-back work on any GoHighLevel plan?**

**A:** Yes, the "Missed Call" trigger is available on all GoHighLevel plans that include an active phone number on the platform. Sending SMS messages consumes message credits, which vary based on your plan and volume. For accounts with high missed-call volume, check your credit balance monthly.

**Q: What if the caller is already in my CRM? Will they receive the message again?**

**A:** Yes, by default the workflow fires for any missed call, regardless of whether the contact is already in the CRM. If you want to avoid sending the message to existing active clients, add a condition filter at the beginning of the workflow that checks whether the contact has an "Active Client" tag. In that case, you can create a branch with a different, more personalized message.

**Q: Can I use missed-call text-back in Spanish or another language to serve non-English-speaking customers?**

**A:** Absolutely. The message text is completely open — you can write it in any language you choose. Many bilingual businesses in the US use one message in English and another in Spanish (or another language), setting up two separate workflows based on the area code of the incoming number, or simply sending a bilingual message in the same SMS.
