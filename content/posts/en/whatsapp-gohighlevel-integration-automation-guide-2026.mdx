## Step-by-Step: How to Connect WhatsApp in GoHighLevel

### 1. Access the Integration Settings

Inside GoHighLevel, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**. You'll see a "Connect WhatsApp via Meta" button. Click it.

### 2. Authenticate Your Meta Business Account

The system will open a Meta pop-up. Log in with the account that has access to your Business Manager. Select the correct business and authorize the requested permissions.

### 3. Create or Select a Phone Number

You can use an existing number (as long as it's not currently active on WhatsApp) or purchase a new one. GHL will prompt you to verify the number via SMS or voice call.

### 4. Set Up Your WhatsApp Business Profile

Add the following:
- Business name
- Business category
- Profile photo (your logo)
- Short description
- Website URL

### 5. Test the Integration

Go to **Conversations**, click the new message icon, select a contact, and choose WhatsApp as the channel. Send a test message. If it goes through — you're live.

### 6. Set Up Message Templates

To send **outbound messages** (where your business initiates the conversation), Meta requires pre-approved Templates. Inside GHL, go to **Settings → WhatsApp → Message Templates** and create your templates. Approval typically takes 24 to 48 hours.

## Common Integration Mistakes (and How to Avoid Them)

**Mistake 1: Using a number that's already active on personal WhatsApp**
Fix: Transfer your personal number to a different SIM or use a brand-new number. The API requires a number with no existing WhatsApp account attached.

**Mistake 2: Skipping template creation before launching campaigns**
Fix: Create and get your templates approved ahead of time. Without an approved template, you can only reply to incoming messages — you can't initiate conversations.

**Mistake 3: Incomplete Meta Business Manager verification**
Fix: Complete your Business Manager verification before starting. Without it, you're capped at just 250 conversations per day.

**Mistake 4: Sending marketing messages without explicit opt-in**
Fix: Meta requires clear consent for marketing messages. Add an opt-in checkbox to your funnel forms before sending any promotional content.

**Mistake 5: Not setting up business hours or an after-hours message**
Fix: Configure an automatic response for outside business hours — so leads don't go hours without any acknowledgment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does WhatsApp in GoHighLevel use the official Meta API?**
**A:** Yes. GoHighLevel's native WhatsApp integration exclusively uses the Official WhatsApp Business API from Meta. This ensures stability, full compliance with Meta's terms of service, and access to all native platform features.

**Q: Do I have to pay Meta to use WhatsApp in GoHighLevel?**
**A:** Yes, but the costs are low. Meta charges per conversation, not per individual message. Conversations initiated by customers (service conversations) are free for the first 1,000 per month. Business-initiated marketing conversations cost an average of $0.025 to $0.08 for US-based contacts.

**Q: Can I use my personal WhatsApp number for the integration?**
**A:** No. Any number currently active on WhatsApp — whether on the personal app or the WhatsApp Business App — must be disconnected before it can be linked to the API. Note that this process will erase the existing conversation history on that number.

**Q: Can I automate responses in both English and Spanish from the same number?**
**A:** Yes. In GoHighLevel, you can build automation workflows that detect a lead's preferred language (via tags, custom fields, or form responses) and send messages in the appropriate language. It's a straightforward configuration inside the Workflow Builder.

**Q: How long does it take for the integration to go live?**
