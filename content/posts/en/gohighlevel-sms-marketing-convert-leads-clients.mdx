## Why SMS Is Still the Most Effective Channel for Service Businesses

SMS — Short Message Service — is plain text messaging. It may be an older technology, but that's precisely why it works so well: every cell phone receives SMS without requiring an app, a Wi-Fi connection, or an account on any platform.

For service businesses — cleaning companies, law firms, medical clinics, consultants — SMS solves three critical problems:

1. **Speed of response:** Industry studies show that 78% of customers go with the first business that responds. SMS arrives in seconds.
2. **Appointment confirmation:** Automated text reminders reduce last-minute cancellations by up to 30%.
3. **Lead follow-up:** Leads who ignore emails will often respond to a simple, direct text message.

Inside GoHighLevel, SMS isn't a separate module — it's part of the same dashboard where you manage contacts, pipelines, emails, and automations. That means every message sent is logged in the client's history, and you can build rules that automatically combine SMS with other channels.

## Step 1 — Set Up Your Phone Number and A2P 10DLC Registration

Before sending any messages, you need an active phone number in GoHighLevel and an approved A2P 10DLC registration. Here's what that means:

**A2P 10DLC** is the US regulatory framework that requires businesses to register their brand and campaigns before sending SMS at volume. Without this registration, carriers will filter or block your messages.

**How to do it:**

1. Open your GoHighLevel dashboard and click **Settings** (the gear icon in the left sidebar).
2. Go to **Phone Numbers** → **Add Number**.
3. Choose a local number with the area code for your city or region (e.g., 305 for Miami, FL; 312 for Chicago, IL).
4. After adding the number, go to **Settings → Compliance → A2P Registration**.
5. You'll see a form with fields for: legal business name, EIN (tax ID number), website, campaign type, and sample messages you plan to send.
6. Fill out all fields carefully — approval takes between 1 and 5 business days.

**What you'll see on screen:** A white-background form divided into sections — Brand Registration and Campaign Registration. Each field has a question-mark tooltip with guidance. At the top, a progress bar shows the steps: Brand → Campaign → Review.

## Step 2 — Create Your First SMS Campaign (Broadcast)

An SMS campaign (called a Broadcast in GoHighLevel) is a bulk send to a segmented list of contacts. It's ideal for promotions, seasonal reminders, or re-engaging inactive clients.

**Step by step:**

1. In the left sidebar, click **Marketing** → **Email & SMS Campaigns** → **+ New Campaign**.
2. Select the type: **SMS**.
3. Give the campaign an internal name (e.g., "Inactive Client Reactivation — October 2026").
4. Under **From**, select your verified phone number.
5. Write your message in the text field. Limit: 160 characters per segment (standard SMS). The system automatically counts remaining characters.
6. Use dynamic variables by clicking **Custom Values**: `{{contact.first_name}}` automatically inserts the contact's first name.
7. Under **Schedule**, choose between sending immediately or scheduling a specific date and time (we recommend business hours: 9 AM–6 PM in the contact's local time zone).
8. Under **Send To**, select your contact list or filter by tag (e.g., "inactive for 60 days").
9. Click **Send Test** to receive a test message on your own phone before confirming.
10. Click **Launch Campaign**.

**What you'll see on screen:** An editor with two panels — settings on the left (name, phone number, schedule) and a message preview on the right inside a mock phone screen. Below the text field, a character counter shows "143/160 characters" in green, turning red when you go over the limit.

### SMS Copywriting Best Practices

- **Always identify yourself:** "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, this is [Business Name]…"
- **One action per message:** Ask for only one thing — confirm, click, or reply.
- **Trackable link:** Use GoHighLevel's built-in link shortener to measure clicks.
- **Opt-out is required:** End with "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" — this is a legal requirement in the US.

## Step 3 — Set Up SMS Automations in the Workflow Builder

While campaigns are one-time sends, SMS automations (inside Workflows) are triggered automatically by lead actions — filling out a form, not responding within 24 hours, booking an appointment, and so on.

**How to create an SMS follow-up automation:**

1. Go to **Automation** → **Workflows** → **+ Create Workflow**.
2. Choose **Start from Scratch** or use a pre-built template (GoHighLevel includes templates like "Lead Follow-Up" and "Appointment Reminder").
3. Click **Add Trigger** and select your trigger. Common examples:
   - **Form Submitted** — when a lead fills out a form on your website
   - **Tag Added** — when a specific tag is added to a contact
   - **Appointment Booked** — when an appointment is scheduled
4. Click **+** to add the first action and select **Send SMS**.
5. Write your message in the text field. You can use the same dynamic variables as in campaigns.
6. To build a sequence, add a **Wait** element (e.g., wait 24 hours) followed by a new **Send SMS** action with a different message.
7. Add **If/Else** branches to create logic: if the contact replied, follow one path; if they didn't, follow another.
8. Activate the workflow by clicking **Publish** in the top right corner.

**What you'll see on screen:** A white canvas with colored blocks connected by arrows. The trigger block is dark blue. SMS action blocks appear in green. Wait blocks appear in gray. Clicking any block opens a right-side panel where you can edit the details.

## Step 4 — Set Up Keyword-Based Auto-Replies

One of the most underused — yet incredibly powerful — features is keyword-based auto-reply. You define specific words that, when a contact texts them back, automatically trigger an action in GoHighLevel.

**Practical use cases:**

| Contact's keyword | Automatic action in GHL |
|---|---|
| "YES" or "CONFIRM" | Confirms the appointment and adds the "Confirmed" tag |
| "STOP" | Removes the contact from SMS lists (legal requirement) |
| "PRICING" or "QUOTE" | Sends a reply with a link to your services page |
| "HELP" | Notifies your team via email or Slack for manual follow-up |

**How to set it up:**

1. In **Automation → Workflows**, create a new workflow.
2. Select the trigger **Inbound SMS**.
3. Click **Filters** and add the condition: **Message Body Contains** → type your keyword (e.g., "YES").
4. Add the corresponding action: **Send SMS**, **Add Tag**, **Update Pipeline Stage**, etc.
5. Save and publish.

## Step 5 — Track Results and Optimize

Setting up campaigns and automations means nothing without monitoring the numbers. GoHighLevel includes a native SMS metrics dashboard:

**Where to find it:**
- Go to **Marketing → Email & SMS Campaigns**.
- Click on the campaign you sent.
- The **Reporting** tab shows: messages delivered, delivery rate, replies received, and link clicks.

**Key metrics to track:**

| Metric | What it indicates | Healthy benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery rate | % of messages that were received | Above 95% |
| Reply rate | % of contacts who responded | Above 8% |
| Click-through rate (if link included) | % who clicked the link | Above 15% |
| Opt-out rate | % who unsubscribed | Below 2% |

If your delivery rate is low, review your A2P registration. If your opt-out rate is high, revisit the frequency and relevance of your messages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I have to pay extra to use SMS in GoHighLevel?**
**A:** GoHighLevel charges based on message volume using a credit system called LC Phone. The cost per SMS in the US is approximately $0.0079 per segment (160 characters). High-volume plans may qualify for lower rates. A2P 10DLC registration has a one-time fee of $4.41 for brand registration and $10.00 per registered campaign.

**Q: What happens if I send SMS without A2P registration?**
**A:** Your messages will likely be filtered by carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) before they ever reach the recipient. You also risk having your number permanently suspended. Since 2023, A2P 10DLC registration has been mandatory for volume SMS sending in the US — it is not optional.

**Q: Can I use GoHighLevel SMS for WhatsApp too?**
**A:** Not directly — SMS and WhatsApp are separate channels. GoHighLevel has a dedicated WhatsApp integration (via the official Meta API) that enables similar automations but requires its own setup. You can use both channels inside the same workflow, routing some contacts via SMS and others via WhatsApp based on their preferences.

**Q: How do I build a contact list for SMS?**
**A:** The most effective approach is capturing phone numbers through forms on your website or sales funnel, with a clear opt-in checkbox ("I agree to receive text messages"). You can also import existing client lists via CSV in GoHighLevel, as long as those contacts have already given prior consent. Never purchase SMS lists — beyond being ineffective, it's illegal under the TCPA in the US.

**Q: What's the best time to send SMS messages to clients?**
**A:** Local business hours — between 9 AM and 6 PM — typically yield the best response rates. Avoid sending before 8 AM or after 8 PM; besides annoying your contacts, it may violate state-level regulations. GoHighLevel lets you schedule sends that respect each individual contact's time zone automatically.
