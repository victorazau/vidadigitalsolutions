## How to Set Up SMS in GoHighLevel: The Basic Flow

If you're just getting started, here's the most straightforward path:

**1. Activate your SMS number**
In GHL, go to *Settings → Phone Numbers* and purchase a local (US) or toll-free number. The LC Phone option (native to GHL) is the simplest way to get started; Twilio offers more control for high-volume sending.

**2. Set up your first Workflow**
Go to *Automation → Workflows → New Workflow*. The most important trigger to start with: *Form Submitted*. Action: send an immediate SMS with a personalized welcome message using the lead's first name.

**3. Build out your follow-up sequence**
After the initial SMS, add a *Wait Step* of 24 hours. If the lead hasn't responded (condition: no reply in conversation), trigger the second SMS. Another 48 hours with no response: send the third and final SMS. After that, tag the lead as "long-term nurture" and move them into an email sequence.

**4. Respect sending time windows**
GHL lets you configure allowed sending hours. Only send SMS during legal hours — typically 8 AM to 9 PM in the lead's time zone. Outside that window, the system holds the message and sends it as soon as the window opens.

**5. Monitor your metrics**
In the *Conversations* dashboard, you can track response rates by campaign. Industry benchmark: if your response rate on the first SMS is below 15%, revisit the message — it's likely too long or too generic.

## Common Mistakes That Kill SMS Marketing in GHL

**Messages that are too long:** SMS has a 160-character limit per segment. Messages over 320 characters cost more and see lower open rates. Be concise and direct.

**Sending SMS outside allowed hours:** Beyond being a poor experience for the lead, it can violate TCPA regulations in certain US states. Always configure your sending window.

**No opt-out option:** Every SMS sequence in the US must include a way to unsubscribe (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe"). GHL can add this automatically to your messages.

**Using SMS for long-form content:** SMS is built for immediate action — scheduling, confirming, replying. For rich content, use email or WhatsApp.

**Keeping SMS disconnected from your CRM:** If SMS lives outside your pipeline, you lose context. In GHL, every SMS reply appears in the contact's timeline — and can automatically move the lead to a new pipeline stage when they respond.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does GoHighLevel SMS work for businesses outside the US?**
**A:** GoHighLevel's native SMS system (LC Phone and Twilio) is optimized for the US and Canada. For businesses or clients in markets where WhatsApp is the dominant channel, GHL supports WhatsApp Business API and covers the same automation workflows. If your operation is US-based but some clients prefer WhatsApp, both integrations can run together within the same workflow.

**Q: Do I need consent before sending automated SMS to leads in the US?**
**A:** Yes. Under US TCPA law, leads must provide express written consent to receive marketing text messages. In practice, this is handled through an opt-in checkbox on your contact form (e.g., "By submitting this form, you agree to receive text messages from [Company Name]"). GoHighLevel has built-in consent fields that can be configured directly on your forms.

**Q: How much does it cost to send SMS through GoHighLevel?**

For high volumes (above 10,000 SMS/month), integrating with Twilio may be more cost-effective. A phone number costs around $1.15/month.

**Q: Can I automatically personalize SMS messages with the lead's name?**
**A:** Absolutely. GoHighLevel uses dynamic variables — called "custom values" or "merge tags" — that pull contact data in real time. You write the message as: "Hey {{contact.first_name}}, we got your inquiry!" — and the system automatically replaces it with each lead's actual name at the moment of sending. This works for name, city, service of interest, and any custom CRM field.
