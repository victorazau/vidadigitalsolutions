## The System That Changes Everything: From First Contact to Follow-Up, Automated

Automation for a cleaning company isn't complicated technology. It's a set of simple processes that run in the background while you work. Here's how it works in practice:

### 1. Missed-Call Text-Back (Automatic Response to Missed Calls)

Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends an automatic SMS within 30 seconds whenever you miss a call. The message can look something like this:

*"Hi! This is [Company Name]. Sorry I missed your call — we're currently with a client. I'd love to help you with cleaning services! Click here to book or get a quote: [link]"*

Typical result: **35% to 50% of leads who didn't get an answer reply to the SMS and continue the booking process.** Without this system, 100% of them disappear.

### 2. Online Scheduling with Automatic Confirmation

A scheduling link integrated with your Google Business Profile and website lets customers choose their own date and time without having to talk to anyone. The system automatically confirms the booking, sends a reminder 24 hours before the appointment, and another one 2 hours before the job.

This eliminates the back-and-forth messaging, reduces no-shows by up to 60%, and frees up hours of your week.

### 3. Automated Post-Quote Follow-Up Sequence

When someone requests a quote but doesn't book right away, 90% of cleaning companies never reach out again. Automation solves this with a sequence of 3 to 5 messages over 7 to 10 days:

- Day 1: Send the quote with a link to book
- Day 3: "Are there still dates available before Thanksgiving I can help you with?"
- Day 7: Offer of the last available slot
- Day 10: "I've kept a spot open for you in January in case that works better!"

This sequence, implemented by Vida Digital Solutions for cleaning companies in Miami and Orlando, produced an average **quote conversion rate increase from 18% to 41%** — with zero additional effort from the business owner.

### 4. Automatic Google Review Request

After every completed job, the system automatically sends a message asking for a Google review. The link opens directly to the review screen — no friction for the customer.

During the holiday season, when hundreds of people are searching "house cleaning near me" before Christmas and Thanksgiving, **having a 4.8-star rating with 80+ Google Maps reviews is the difference between showing up in the top 3 results or not showing up at all.**
