## Why AI Recommends Some Businesses and Ignores Others

AI assistants like ChatGPT are trained on publicly available data from the internet. When someone asks for a cleaning company in Miami, the system looks for patterns: which businesses appear consistently? Which ones have positive, recent reviews? Which ones have websites that clearly describe their services, geographic area, and what makes them different?

This is the logic behind AEO — Answer Engine Optimization. It's not just about ranking on Google anymore. It's about being the answer the AI gives when a potential client asks.

**To show up in AI results, your business needs:**

- **A website with clear, specific language** — not just "cleaning services," but "residential and commercial cleaning in Broward County, FL, with online scheduling"
- **An active Google Business Profile** — new photos every week, accurate hours, responses to every review

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does ChatGPT actually recommend local cleaning companies by name?**
**A:** Yes. AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini already answer questions about local services and cite real businesses by name when there's enough public data available — such as a website, Google reviews, a Google Business Profile, and online mentions. Businesses without a structured digital presence simply don't appear in those answers.

**Q: How many Google reviews does a cleaning business need to rank in local search?**
**A:** There's no fixed minimum, but businesses that appear in the top 3 on Google Maps in mid-size US cities typically have between 25 and 80 reviews with an average rating above 4.5 stars. More important than the total number is frequency: receiving 2 to 4 new reviews per month signals to Google and AI systems that your business is active and trustworthy.

**Q: Do I need a website if I already have Instagram and a Google Business Profile?**
**A:** Yes. Instagram is not indexed by Google the same way a website is, and AI assistants don't learn well from social media profiles. A website with clear service pages, a defined service area, and an FAQ section is the primary asset that gets your business recognized by AI systems and ranked in organic Google search. Your Google Business Profile and Instagram complement your website — they don't replace it.

**Q: How long does it take for a cleaning business to appear in AI results after building out its digital presence?**
**A:** Initial improvements in Google Maps rankings typically appear within 30 to 60 days with a well-optimized Google Business Profile and consistent reviews. Appearing in AI assistant responses like ChatGPT usually takes 60 to 120 days, because those systems update their data less frequently than Google does. Consistency matters more than speed.

**Q: Does review request automation work for small businesses with only 1 or 2 employees?**
**A:** Yes — and honestly, that's where it makes the biggest difference. Small businesses are the ones with the least time to ask for reviews manually, and they're also the ones who need reviews most to compete with larger operations. A simple SMS or WhatsApp automation, configured once, collects reviews consistently with zero additional effort from you or your team.
