## What Is GoHighLevel Calendar and How Does It Work

GoHighLevel Calendar is the scheduling module built directly into the GoHighLevel platform — a CRM and automation system designed for small and mid-sized service businesses. It lets you create different types of calendars: individual (one professional), team (the client picks an available team member), and service-based (with set durations and pricing per service type).

The real differentiator is what happens **after** a booking is made. When a lead schedules a consultation through your GoHighLevel Calendar link, the platform automatically executes a sequence of actions:

1. The lead is moved to the "Consultation Scheduled" stage in your sales pipeline
2. A confirmation is sent via email and SMS immediately
3. Reminders are triggered 24 hours and 1 hour before the appointment
4. If the client doesn't show up, a rescheduling workflow fires automatically
5. After the appointment, a satisfaction survey or review request can be sent

## Calendly: Simple, Popular, and With Serious Limitations

Calendly is, without a doubt, the most well-known scheduling tool on the market. It's easy to set up, has a clean interface, and works great for one specific use case: **booking meetings between professionals**. Consultants, sales reps, and coaches love Calendly because clients just click a link, pick a time, and they're done.

**Where Calendly shines:**
- Set up in under 15 minutes
- Integrates with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Zoom
- Intuitive booking experience for clients
- Functional free plan for basic use

**Where Calendly falls short for service businesses:**
- No native CRM — every booking exists in isolation
- Limited automations: connecting to your sales pipeline requires paying for Zapier or Make
- No integrated lead pipeline management
- Advanced reminders (SMS) only available on higher-priced paid plans
- Doesn't consolidate marketing, sales, and service data in one place

The practical result: a law firm using Calendly to schedule consultations has to manually copy booking data into the CRM, send reminders separately, and manage follow-up on their own — or pay for multiple integrations that inevitably break.

## Acuity Scheduling: Solid for Service Businesses With Variable Pricing

Acuity Scheduling (acquired by Squarespace in 2019) is a more complete option than Calendly, especially for businesses with multiple services and different price points. Salons, pilates studios, medical clinics, and photographers often prefer Acuity because it lets you build a "service menu" with a set duration and price for each offering.

**Acuity's strengths:**
- Multiple service types with different prices and durations
- Integrated payments via Stripe and PayPal
- Custom intake forms before booking
- Automatic email reminders (SMS via paid add-on)
- Strong integration with Squarespace for existing users

**Acuity's limitations:**
- Also operates separately from CRM and sales pipeline
- The client-facing interface isn't as modern as Calendly or GoHighLevel
- Connecting to your automation system requires Zapier
- Costs can climb quickly with add-ons

Acuity is a solid tool — but it's still just one piece of the client management puzzle. You'll still need a separate CRM, a separate email marketing system, and a separate pipeline manager.

## How to Set Up GoHighLevel Calendar in Practice

For anyone evaluating the tool, here's an overview of what the setup involves:

**Step 1 — Choose your calendar type**
Select from event calendar, service calendar, team calendar, or round-robin (automatically distributes bookings among team members).

**Step 2 — Set availability and buffer times**
Configure your available hours, the minimum advance notice required for booking, and the buffer between appointments (e.g., 15 minutes for preparation).

**Step 3 — Build your booking form**
Add the fields you need before the consultation: name, phone number, service type, how they found you. That data flows directly into the contact record in your CRM.

**Step 4 — Set up post-booking automations**
In the Workflow Builder, create your sequence: instant confirmation → 24-hour reminder → 1-hour reminder → post-appointment follow-up. You can use email, SMS, or WhatsApp — all within GoHighLevel.

**Step 5 — Embed the calendar on your website or funnel**
GoHighLevel generates a public link and an embed code to place on your website or funnel page. Clients can book without needing to call or send a message.
