## How to Set Up GoHighLevel Calendar for Service Businesses

The basic setup can be completed in under 2 hours. Here's the recommended flow:

### 1. Choose Your Calendar Type

GoHighLevel offers three main calendar types:

| Type | Best For | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Round Robin** | Teams with multiple staff members | Automatically distributes bookings among available team members |
| **Individual** | Solo professionals or specific consultations | Books directly with one designated professional |
| **Class Booking** | Groups, workshops, courses | Allows multiple participants in the same time slot |

For most service businesses — clinics, attorneys, cleaning providers — the **Individual** or **Round Robin** calendar covers 90% of use cases.

### 2. Configure Availability and Rules

- Set your working days and hours
- Configure buffer time between appointments (prevents the next client from arriving while the previous one is still being served)
- Enable a daily booking limit to avoid overloading your team
- Set a minimum advance notice requirement (e.g., "bookings must be made at least 4 hours in advance")

### 3. Activate Reminder Automations

This is the step that dramatically cuts no-shows. Configure:

- **Immediate confirmation** after booking (email + SMS)
- **24-hour reminder** with an option to reschedule
- **1–2 hour reminder** with the address or access link (for virtual consultations)
- **Post-appointment follow-up** to collect feedback or schedule a return visit

If you want to understand how these automations connect to client onboarding, see [how to automate client onboarding in GoHighLevel](/blog/how-to-automate-client-onboarding-gohighlevel).

### 4. Connect to Your Lead Pipeline

The major advantage of GoHighLevel Calendar over standalone tools: when someone books, you can configure a workflow that:

- Automatically creates the contact in your CRM
- Moves the lead to the "Consultation Scheduled" stage in the pipeline
- Triggers a pre-appointment email nurture sequence
- Assigns a preparation task to the responsible team member

This transforms scheduling from an administrative action into an active step in your sales funnel.
