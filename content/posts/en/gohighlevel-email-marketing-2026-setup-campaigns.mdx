## Step 1 — Set Up Your Sending Domain (The Most Important Step)

Before sending any campaign, you need to authenticate your sending domain. Without this setup, your emails have a high chance of landing in spam — especially in Gmail and Outlook, which account for over 70% of inboxes in the US.

Domain authentication is the process of proving to email providers (Google, Microsoft) that you are the legitimate owner of the domain you're sending from. It's done through three DNS records:

**SPF (Sender Policy Framework):** defines which servers are authorized to send emails on behalf of your domain.

**DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail):** a digital signature that proves the email wasn't altered between sending and delivery.

**DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication):** a policy that instructs the email provider on what to do when an email fails SPF or DKIM verification.

**How to configure this in GoHighLevel:**
1. Go to **Settings → Email Services → Dedicated Domain & IP**
2. Add your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com)
3. GHL will generate the DNS records you need to add in your domain registrar's panel (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.)
4. Wait for propagation (typically 24–48 hours) and click "Verify"
5. Once verified, all emails will go out from your authenticated domain

This single step can boost your deliverability rate from 60% to over 95%. It's the step that Vida Digital Solutions makes mandatory in every GoHighLevel implementation for our clients.

## Step 2 — Build Your Contact List with Tag-Based Segmentation

In GoHighLevel, you don't work with isolated "lists" the way you do in Mailchimp. You work with **contacts in the CRM** and organize them using **tags** and **smart lists**.

This approach is far more powerful because the same contact can receive different emails depending on where they are in your funnel. A lead who requested a quote gets one sequence. A client who signed on gets another. A client who's been inactive for 90 days gets a re-engagement campaign.

**How to create a Smart List for email:**
1. Go to **Contacts → Smart Lists**
2. Click "Add Smart List"
3. Set your filters (e.g., tag = "hot-lead", source = "website form", date created = last 30 days)
4. Save the list — it updates automatically as new contacts match the criteria

**Essential tags for service businesses:**
- `new-lead` — someone who just filled out a form
- `quote-sent` — received a proposal, hasn't signed yet
- `active-client` — signed on and currently active
- `inactive-client` — hasn't purchased in more than 60 days
- `re-engagement` — candidate for a reactivation campaign

This tag structure feeds both broadcast campaigns (manual sends) and automated workflows — as we cover in detail in our guide on the [GoHighLevel Workflow Builder](/blog/gohighlevel-workflow-builder-complete-guide-2026).

## Step 3 — Set Up Email Campaigns: Broadcast vs. Workflow

In GoHighLevel, you have two ways to send emails:

**Broadcast (manual campaign):** you select a list or tag, write the email, set the date and time, and send. Ideal for newsletters, seasonal promotions, and product or service announcements.

**Workflow (automated sequence):** the email is triggered automatically when a contact takes an action (filled out a form, didn't respond within X days, opened the previous email but didn't click). Ideal for nurturing, onboarding, and re-engagement.

### How to Create a Broadcast:
1. Go to **Marketing → Email Marketing → Campaigns**
2. Click "Create Campaign"
3. Choose a template or build from scratch using the drag-and-drop editor
4. Select the Smart List of recipients
5. Configure the subject line, preview text, and sender name
6. Schedule it or send immediately

### How to Create an Automated Sequence via Workflow:
1. Go to **Automation → Workflows**
2. Click "Create Workflow"
3. Set the trigger (e.g., "Form Submitted" — quote request form filled out)
4. Add the "Send Email" action and choose your template
5. Add a "Wait" step of 2 days, then add a follow-up email
6. Continue the sequence as needed

This is the structure Vida Digital Solutions uses in automation projects for service businesses: 3 to 5 automatic emails after the first contact, with strategic intervals and progressive content that moves the lead from interest to decision.

## Step 4 — Copywriting Best Practices for High Open Rates

Technical setup handles deliverability. But what actually gets emails opened — and drives results — is the copy.

**Subject line:** this is the single biggest factor in your open rate. Avoid words like "free", "limited offer", "act now" — spam filters flag these patterns. Use conversational, specific language instead:
- ❌ "Special offer just for you!"
- ✅ "Still thinking about automating your scheduling?"
- ✅ "Hey [First Name], I saw you requested a quote last week"

**Preview text:** the snippet that appears right after the subject line in the inbox. Use it to complement the subject — don't just repeat it.

**Opening paragraph:** immediately tell the reader what they'll get from this email. Nobody reads a business email for fun — it's a utility read.

**Single CTA:** every email should have one goal and one action button. Two or more CTAs split attention and reduce conversions.

**Benchmark metrics for service businesses in 2026:**
- Healthy open rate: 25–40%
- Healthy click rate: 2–5%
- Acceptable unsubscribe rate: below 0.5%
- Acceptable spam complaint rate: below 0.08%

## Step 5 — Monitor, Test, and Optimize

GoHighLevel provides detailed per-campaign reports: opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and bounces. Use this data to make decisions — not just to look at numbers.

**A/B Testing Subject Lines:** GHL lets you test two versions of a subject line with a sample of your list before sending to everyone. Use this whenever possible — improvements of 20–30% in open rates are common with a well-structured test.

**List hygiene:** contacts who haven't opened any emails in 6 months hurt your domain reputation. Create a "inactive" Smart List and send a re-engagement campaign. Anyone who doesn't respond should be removed.

**Send timing:** for B2C service businesses in the US, the best send times are generally Tuesday through Thursday, between 9 AM and 11 AM in the recipient's time zone. Test with your specific audience to confirm.
