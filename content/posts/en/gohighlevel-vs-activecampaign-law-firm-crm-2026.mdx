## Where ActiveCampaign Still Wins

Honesty matters in any comparison — and ActiveCampaign has real advantages in specific contexts:

**1. Highly segmented email automations**
If your firm sends monthly newsletters to a list of 20,000 contacts segmented by practice area (immigration, employment law, family law), ActiveCampaign delivers more granular segmentation logic natively. GoHighLevel handles it well, but it's not where the platform shines.

**2. Behavior-based lead scoring**
ActiveCampaign's lead scoring is more mature — it scores leads based on email opens, clicks, and on-site behavior in greater detail. For firms with very high inbound lead volume, this can be relevant.

**3. Email marketing reporting**
If you need detailed reports on open rates, per-link click tracking, email heat maps, and campaign comparisons, ActiveCampaign still has the edge in email data depth.

**The real question is:** do these advantages actually solve the core problem for a law firm? The real problem for 90% of law firms isn't advanced email segmentation — it's **not responding to leads fast enough, not having automated scheduling, and not following up after the initial consultation**. And that's exactly where GoHighLevel dominates.
