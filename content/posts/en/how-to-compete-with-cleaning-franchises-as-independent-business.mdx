### 2. Optimized Google Business Profile

When someone in your area searches *"house cleaning near me"*, who shows up first on the map? Franchises invest heavily in local SEO — but you can absolutely dominate your zip code if you optimize your profile correctly.

The most important ranking factors in 2026:

1. **Real, up-to-date photos** — at least 20 before-and-after shots, team photos, and product photos
2. **Consistent reviews** — Google prioritizes profiles with a steady flow of new reviews, not one-time spikes
3. **Weekly posts** — the algorithm favors active profiles
4. **Correct categories** — "House Cleaning Service" + "Maid Service" + "Janitorial Service"
5. **Responding to every review** — shows that your business is real and that you care

A business with 80+ reviews and an active profile frequently ranks **above** franchises with less local engagement.

### 3. Review Automation

Reviews are the currency of the cleaning market. An independent business with a 4.9-star rating and 120 reviews looks more trustworthy than a franchise with 3.8 stars and 40 reviews.

The problem with asking for reviews manually is that you remember to do it half the time, the client forgets, and your review count stalls. With automation:

- **24 hours after the service**, the client receives an automatic SMS: *"Hi! How was your cleaning today? If everything went great, would you mind leaving us a quick review? It helps a lot! [direct Google link]"*
- If they don't respond, they receive a gentle reminder at 48 hours
- Negative feedback is captured privately first, giving you the chance to resolve it before it becomes a public one-star review

This is exactly what franchises do — and you can set it up in a weekend with GoHighLevel.
