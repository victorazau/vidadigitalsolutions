## What Every Cleaning Business Owner Should Require from Their Software

Before choosing any tool, make sure it solves these 5 things:

1. **Instant response to new leads** — automation that replies in under 2 minutes, 24 hours a day
2. **Self-service scheduling** — clients book their own appointment without any back-and-forth
3. **Automatic reminders** — SMS 24 hours before the service (reduces cancellations significantly)
4. **Post-service follow-up** — automatic message checking in after the visit and requesting a review
5. **Organized client history** — know who's a recurring client, when their last visit was, and what they paid

If the tool doesn't solve all 5, you'll end up running another tool alongside it — which creates more work, not less.
