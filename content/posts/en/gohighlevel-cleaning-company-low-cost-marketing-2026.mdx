## Step 1 — Set Up Missed-Call Text-Back (The Most Important Automation)

Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends an automatic SMS whenever you miss an incoming call. For cleaning businesses, this is gold: while you're on your knees scrubbing a bathroom, GoHighLevel has already responded to the potential client who called asking about pricing.

Industry studies show that **78% of customers hire the first company that responds**. If you take hours to call back, that client has already booked your competitor.

**How to set it up in GoHighLevel:**

1. From the main dashboard, go to the left sidebar and click on **Automations** (the lightning bolt icon).
2. Click the blue **+ Create Workflow** button in the top right corner.
3. Select **Start from Scratch** and click **Create New Workflow**.
4. In the workflow editor, click **+ Add New Trigger**.
5. From the trigger list, select **Missed Call** — you'll see a description that reads "Triggered when an incoming call is missed".
6. Click **+ Add New Action** just below the trigger.
7. Select **Send SMS** from the action list.
8. In the message field, write something like: *"Hey! This is [Business Name]. I saw you called and couldn't pick up. Feel free to reply here — are you looking for residential or commercial cleaning? I'll get you a quote right away!"*
9. Click **Save Action** and then hit **Save** in the top right corner.
10. Activate the workflow by toggling **Published** — it will turn green.

> **💡 Tip:** Include your business name in the SMS and keep the tone friendly. Messages that sound too formal come across as robotic and people won't respond.

## Step 2 — Build a Lead Pipeline So No Quote Ever Slips Through

A pipeline is the visual board where you track every lead at every stage — from first contact all the way to payment. Without it, quote requests get buried in your inbox and you forget to follow up.

If you want to understand how to build a pipeline from scratch, check out the full tutorial at [How to Create a Sales Pipeline in GoHighLevel](/blog/how-to-create-sales-pipeline-gohighlevel).

**How to build a basic cleaning pipeline:**

1. In the left sidebar, click **CRM** and then **Pipelines**.
2. Click **+ Add Pipeline** and name it "Cleaning Quotes".
3. Add the following columns (stages) by clicking **+ Add Stage**:
   - **New Lead** — contact came in, not yet responded
   - **Quote Sent** — you've already sent pricing
   - **Awaiting Confirmation** — client is thinking it over
   - **Scheduled** — date and time confirmed
   - **Service Completed** — cleaning is done
   - **Review Requested** — asked for a Google review
4. Click **Save Pipeline**.

Now every time a lead comes in — via form, phone call, or DM — you create a card and drag it forward as it progresses. In 30 seconds you can see exactly where every client stands.

> **💡 Tip:** Set up an automation that automatically moves a card to "New Lead" when a form is submitted from your website. That way you never have to create cards manually.

## Step 3 — Activate Automatic Google Review Requests

Google reviews are the most powerful free marketing available for cleaning businesses. A company with 80 five-star reviews closes three times more clients than one with 10 reviews — without spending a dime on ads.

The problem is that most cleaning business owners forget to ask for a review after the job is done. GoHighLevel handles that automatically.

**How to set it up:**

1. Go to **Automations → + Create Workflow → Start from Scratch**.
2. Add the trigger **Pipeline Stage Changed**.
3. Under **Filter**, select the "Cleaning Quotes" pipeline and the "Service Completed" stage.
4. Add a **Wait** action — set it to wait **2 hours** (enough time for the client to get home and settle in).
5. Add a **Send SMS** action with this message: *"Hi [First Name]! I hope you're loving the results 😊 If you have a moment, would you mind leaving us a Google review? It means the world to our team: [your Google Business Profile review link]"*.
6. Add another **Wait** of **3 days**, then a **Send Email** action with a gentle follow-up for clients who haven't left a review yet.
7. Save and publish the workflow.

> **💡 Tip:** Find your direct Google review link by going to your Google Business Profile, clicking "Share" → "Copy review link". That's the link you'll paste into the SMS.

## Step 4 — Create a Win-Back Sequence for Past Clients

Past clients are the cheapest leads you'll ever get. They already know you, they've already trusted you — they just need a little nudge. A win-back sequence is a series of messages sent automatically to clients who haven't booked in the last 30, 60, or 90 days.

This is one of the strategies Vida Digital Solutions uses with cleaning company clients to fill their calendars without spending on ads.

**How to set up the win-back sequence:**

1. Create a new workflow with the trigger **Tag Added**.
2. Create a tag called "Reactivate 60 Days" — you'll apply this tag manually or via automation to clients who have gone inactive.
3. Add the following sequence of actions:
   - **Immediately:** Send SMS — *"Hey [First Name], it's been a while! We've got some openings this week — want to get a cleaning on the books?"*
   - **Wait 3 days:** Send Email — subject line "Your home deserves a deep clean 🏠" with a special offer for returning clients.
   - **Wait 4 days:** Send SMS — *"Last call! We still have a few spots available this week. Let me know if you're interested and I'll hold one for you."*
4. Save and publish.

This 3-message sequence over 7 days reactivates an average of **20 to 30% of inactive clients**, at no additional cost beyond your GoHighLevel subscription.

## Step 5 — Set Up a Quote Request Form on Your Website

If you don't have a form on your website connected directly to GoHighLevel, you're losing leads every week. When someone fills it out, they automatically enter your CRM, a card is created in your pipeline, and a welcome message is sent — all without you lifting a finger.

**How to create and install the form:**

1. In the left sidebar, click **Sites → Forms → + Create Form**.
2. Select **Blank Form** and name it "Cleaning Quote Request".
3. Add fields by dragging them from the sidebar:
   - Full Name
   - Phone
   - Email
   - Dropdown: "Type of service" (Residential / Commercial / Move-In/Out / Airbnb)
   - Text area: "Address and additional notes"
4. Under **On Submit**, configure the **Go To URL** action to point to a thank-you page.
5. Click **Save**, then go to **Integrate → Copy Embed Code**.
6. Paste the code into your website (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace — all support HTML embeds).

> **💡 Tip:** Create a workflow with the trigger **Form Submitted** for this form. The first action should be an immediate SMS: *"Hi [First Name]! We received your quote request and will get back to you within the hour. Thanks for reaching out!"* This instantly positions you as a fast, professional business.

With this data, you know exactly where to focus your energy — and where to stop wasting time.
