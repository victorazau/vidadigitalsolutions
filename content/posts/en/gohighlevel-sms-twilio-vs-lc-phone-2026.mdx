## What Is LC Phone and What Is Twilio in GoHighLevel?

Before comparing, let's define each one:

**LC Phone (Lead Connector Phone)** is HighLevel's native telephony provider — the company acquired its own telecom infrastructure and launched an in-house solution. You manage everything inside the GoHighLevel dashboard without creating an account on any external service.

**Twilio** is an independent cloud communications platform — one of the largest in the world — that GoHighLevel relied on as its sole provider until 2022, when LC Phone launched as an alternative. If you choose Twilio, you create an account at twilio.com, connect it via API to GoHighLevel, and manage credits and numbers there.

The question Vida Digital Solutions gets most often from new clients is exactly this: "Do I need to create a Twilio account, or does everything come with GoHighLevel?" The answer: **no, you no longer need Twilio** — LC Phone handles it for most use cases.

### Deliverability and A2P 10DLC Registration

In 2026, the biggest factor in SMS deliverability in the US isn't the provider — it's **A2P 10DLC registration** (Application-to-Person, 10-Digit Long Code). Without proper registration, your messages get filtered by carriers and never reach the recipient.

Both LC Phone and Twilio require this registration. The difference is where you do it:

- **LC Phone:** the process lives inside GoHighLevel itself (Settings → Phone Numbers → A2P Registration). More intuitive, with HighLevel support available if you get stuck.
- **Twilio:** you register directly in the Twilio dashboard following their workflow. More technical control, but more complex.

Once properly registered, **deliverability is equivalent** between both providers — they both use the same carriers (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon).

## When to Choose LC Phone

Choose **LC Phone** if you:

- Are getting started with GoHighLevel now
- Run a single business (not an agency with dozens of clients)
- Want to get set up in minutes without relying on outside technical support
- Prefer managing everything in one dashboard
- Send moderate SMS volumes (up to ~50,000 messages/month)
- Want unified support — one problem, one place to resolve it

## When to Choose Twilio

Choose **Twilio** if you:

- Run a GoHighLevel agency with 20+ clients and want to centralize telephony billing
- Need short codes for high-volume campaigns
- Have a technical team already using the Twilio API in other systems
- Operate in multiple countries outside the US and Canada
- Need advanced voice features (detailed call tracking, custom IVR)
- Negotiate volume discounts above 100,000 messages/month

## Migrating from Twilio to LC Phone: Is It Worth It?

If you're already using Twilio in GoHighLevel and considering a migration, the answer depends on your situation:

**Migrate if:**
- You're spending time managing two separate dashboards
- Your team struggles to troubleshoot SMS issues because they can't tell if the problem is in GHL or Twilio
- Your volume doesn't justify price negotiations with Twilio

**Don't migrate if:**
- You have active short codes with registered campaigns
- You use the Twilio API in integrations outside of GoHighLevel
- You have custom pricing agreements that outweigh the added complexity

The migration itself is straightforward: you purchase new numbers in LC Phone, update your workflows, and cancel your Twilio numbers. The biggest task is **re-registering for A2P 10DLC** with the new provider — a process that takes 5 to 15 business days depending on the carrier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use LC Phone and Twilio at the same time in GoHighLevel?**
**A:** No — you choose one telephony provider per sub-account in GoHighLevel. You cannot mix LC Phone and Twilio within the same account. Agencies managing multiple clients can have different providers across different sub-accounts, but each sub-account uses only one.

**Q: Is A2P 10DLC registration required to send SMS in 2026?**
**A:** Yes, it's mandatory for Application-to-Person SMS sending in the US since 2023. Without registration, carriers will filter or block your messages. Whether you're on LC Phone or Twilio, you must register your brand and campaign before sending SMS at scale. The process typically takes 5 to 15 business days.

**Q: Which provider has better support when something goes wrong?**
**A:** For GoHighLevel users without a technical team, LC Phone offers simpler support — you open a ticket in GHL and they handle everything. With Twilio, issues may require coordination between GHL support and Twilio support, which slows down resolution. For agencies with developers, Twilio's technical support is more robust and well-documented.

**Q: Does LC Phone support SMS sending in Canada as well?**
**A:** Yes, LC Phone supports numbers and SMS sending in both the US and Canada. For other countries, coverage is limited and Twilio tends to be more comprehensive. If your operations are focused on the North American market, LC Phone covers you well.

**Q: How long does it take to set up LC Phone from scratch in GoHighLevel?**
**A:** The basic setup — adding credits, purchasing a number, and sending your first SMS — takes less than 10 minutes. A2P 10DLC registration, which is required for sending at volume, takes 5 to 15 business days depending on carrier approval. During that period, low-volume test sends are still possible.
