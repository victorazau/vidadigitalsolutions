## Why Google Ads Alone Won't Close Clients

Legal advertising is one of the most expensive categories in paid search. Keywords like *immigration lawyer near me* or *personal injury attorney* can cost anywhere from $40 to $200 per click depending on the city. A mid-sized law firm in the US can easily spend $5,000 to $15,000 per month on paid traffic alone.

The problem? Most firms are wasting a significant portion of that investment.

When a lead fills out your website form or calls your ad's phone number after business hours, what happens? If the answer is "they wait until the next business day," you've already lost that client. They searched two more competitors before going to bed and most likely booked a consultation with whoever responded first.

**The Google Ads funnel for law firms has three critical breaking points:**

1. **The lead submits the form and gets no immediate confirmation** — they disengage due to silence.
2. **Your team tries to call the next morning** — the lead has already gone cold or hired another firm.
3. **No structured follow-up after the first contact attempt** — hot leads become dead leads within 48 hours.

None of these problems are solved by increasing your ad budget. All of them are solved with CRM automation.
