## 1. Create a "Holiday Cleaning" Pipeline in GoHighLevel

The first step is getting a clear visual of where every seasonal lead stands in your process. A dedicated pipeline keeps holiday deep cleaning requests from getting mixed in with your regular clients and lost in the shuffle.

**How to do it:**

1. In the GoHighLevel left-hand menu, go to **CRM → Pipelines** and click **+ Add Pipeline**.
2. Name it `Holiday Cleaning 2026` and add the following stages:
   - **New Request** — lead just came in (form, Instagram DM, phone call).
   - **Quote Sent** — estimate sent, waiting for a response.
   - **Booked** — job scheduled and confirmed.
   - **Service Done** — cleaning completed.
   - **Review Requested** — review request sent to the client.
   - **Recurring Follow-up** — client entered the reactivation sequence for January.
3. Click **Save** and set this pipeline as your default for the next 60 days.

**What you'll see on screen:** a Kanban-style board with color-coded columns. Drag client cards between columns as their status progresses — or let your workflows handle it automatically (covered in the next steps).

> 💡 **Pro tip:** create a custom field called `Service Type` with the options *Deep Cleaning*, *Move-out*, *Pre-party*, *Post-party*, and *Airbnb Turnover*. This lets you filter reports and personalize automated messages by service type.

## 2. Automate Your Instant Lead Response (Under 2 Minutes)

Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends an automatic SMS when you miss a call — but this step goes further: any new lead (form submission, missed call, or DM) gets a response in under 2 minutes, 24 hours a day.

This matters because **78% of clients book with the first company that responds**. During the holidays, cleaning business owners are out running jobs — they can't stop to type out messages.

**How to do it:**

1. Go to **Automation → Workflows** and click **+ Create Workflow**.
2. Choose **Start from Scratch** and name it: `Holiday Lead — Instant Reply`.
3. **Trigger:** select `Contact Created` and add the filter `Pipeline = Holiday Cleaning 2026`.
4. **Action 1 — Immediate SMS:** add the `Send SMS` action with the following message:
   > *"Hi [Contact.FirstName]! Thanks for reaching out to [Business Name]. We'd love to help with your holiday cleaning 🎄 We'll send your quote within the hour. Any questions? Reply here anytime!"*
5. **Action 2 — Move in pipeline:** add `Move to Pipeline Stage → New Request` to make sure the card shows up immediately on your Kanban board.
6. **Action 3 — Internal notification:** add `Send Internal Notification` to alert you (or your team) via email or the app about the new lead.
7. Toggle the workflow on and click **Publish**.

**What you'll see on screen:** the workflow displays as a flowchart with arrows connecting each action. Each block is color-coded — triggers in blue, actions in green, conditions in yellow.

## 3. Set Up Your Quote Follow-up Sequence (SMS + Email)

Sending the quote is only half the job. Most cleaning companies lose the client because they don't follow up consistently — the prospect goes quiet and books with someone else. This automated sequence fixes that.

**How to do it:**

1. Create a new workflow: `Holiday Quote — Follow-up Sequence`.
2. **Trigger:** `Pipeline Stage Changed → Quote Sent`.
3. Build the action sequence using Wait steps:

| Time after quote is sent | Channel | Key message |
|---|---|---|
| 0 min | Email | Quote attached + service summary |
| 4 hours | SMS | *"Hi [Name], just checking in — did you get our quote? Happy to adjust anything 😊"* |
| 1 day | Email | Client testimonials + link to book |
| 2 days | SMS | *"Holiday slots are filling up fast — want to lock in your date?"* |
| 4 days | SMS | *"Last follow-up from us, [Name]. If you're still interested, reply YES and we'll hold your spot!"* |

4. Add an `If/Else` condition before each message: **if the stage has already changed to Booked, stop the sequence**. This prevents follow-ups from going out to clients who already booked.
5. To add the condition: click the `+` between actions → choose `If/Else` → condition: `Pipeline Stage = Booked` → *Yes* branch: `End Workflow`.

> 💡 **Pro tip:** enable GoHighLevel's `Conversation AI` in this sequence so client replies are handled automatically during business hours. The AI answers simple questions (pricing, availability) and escalates to you when needed. ## 4. Automate Your Post-Service Review Request

Holiday reviews are gold. Clients who are thrilled with their pre-Christmas or post-party cleaning are at peak satisfaction — it's the perfect moment to ask for a Google review. And GoHighLevel automates the entire process.

**How to do it:**

1. Create the workflow: `Post-Service — Review Request`.
2. **Trigger:** `Pipeline Stage Changed → Service Done`.
3. **Action 1 — Wait 2 hours:** give clients a moment to settle in after the service before asking for a review.
4. **Action 2 — SMS:** send:
   > *"Hi [Name]! Hope you're loving the results 🌟 Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It helps our small business a ton: [Google Review Link]. Thank you so much!"*
5. **Action 3 — Wait 2 days:** if no review has been left yet.
6. **Action 4 — Email:** a more complete version with a team photo, a thank-you note, and the review link.
7. **Action 5 — Move to Recurring Follow-up:** move the card to the final pipeline stage, setting the client up for the January reactivation sequence.

**To get your Google Review link:** go to your Google Business Profile → `Ask for Reviews` → copy the direct link. Paste it as a fixed variable in the SMS.

## 5. Plant Your January Follow-up Now (Client Reactivation)

This step is what separates cleaning business owners who kick off January with a full schedule from those who spend the first few weeks of the year waiting for the phone to ring. The idea is simple: every client who books a job in November or December is automatically added to a sequence that sends them the right message in January.

**How to do it:**

1. Create the workflow: `January Reactivation — Cleaning`.
2. **Trigger:** `Pipeline Stage Changed → Recurring Follow-up`.
3. **Action 1 — Wait until date:** set it to wait until **January 6, 2027** (the first full business week after the holidays).
4. **Action 2 — SMS:**
   > *"Happy New Year, [Name]! 🎉 Starting the year with a clean home is the best feeling. We have a few spots open in January — want to schedule your next cleaning? Reply YES and we'll send you the available dates!"*
5. **Action 3 — Wait 3 days:**
6. **Action 4 — Email:** send a recurring service package offer (e.g., 10% off for clients who sign up for monthly cleanings).
7. **Action 5 — Tag:** add the tag `January-2027-Lead` to filter and track who responded.

**What you'll see on screen:** in the workflow, the `Wait until date` block displays with a calendar icon. You set the exact date and time — we recommend Tuesday or Wednesday at 9:00 AM in the client's time zone.
