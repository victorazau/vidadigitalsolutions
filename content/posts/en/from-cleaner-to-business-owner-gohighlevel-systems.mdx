## Step 1 — Set Up Your Lead Pipeline (The Backbone of Your Business)

The **pipeline** in GoHighLevel is a visual map showing where each client is in the buying process. For a cleaning business, a solid basic pipeline has 5 stages:

| Stage | What It Means |
|---|---|
| New Lead | Made first contact |
| Quote Sent | You've already sent pricing |
| Waiting on Decision | Client is thinking it over |
| Scheduled | Service is booked |
| Recurring Client | Completed first service and became a repeat customer |

**How to set it up:**
1. Open GoHighLevel and click on **Opportunities** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click **+ Add Pipeline** in the upper right corner.
3. Name it *Cleaning Business* and add the 5 stages above by clicking **+ Add Stage** for each one.
4. Assign a different color to each stage (green for scheduled, yellow for waiting, red for new leads without a response).
5. Click **Save Pipeline**.

**What you'll see on screen:** A Kanban-style board with color-coded cards for each lead. When you drag a card from "New Lead" to "Quote Sent," the system can automatically fire off a message — which you'll configure in the next step.

## Step 2 — Create an Instant Lead Response Automation

An **instant lead response** is an automation that sends a pre-written message within 2 minutes of someone filling out a form or sending a message — even if your hands are covered in cleaning supplies.

Industry studies show that **78% of clients book with the first business that responds**. Being fast isn't a competitive advantage — it's the minimum standard.

**How to set it up:**
1. In the left-hand menu, go to **Automation → Workflows → + Create Workflow**.
2. Select the *Lead Nurture — Contact Created* template or start from scratch.
3. In the first block, choose your **Trigger:** *Form Submitted* (for leads from your website) or *Inbound Message* (for direct messages).
4. Click **+ Add Action → Send SMS** and write your message. Example:
   > *"Hi! This is [Business Name]. I got your message and I'm already checking availability for you. I'll follow up shortly with all the details! 😊"*
5. Add a second **Action → Send Email** with a more detailed email introducing your services.
6. Add a **Wait Step** of 30 minutes, then a follow-up SMS with your scheduling link.
7. Click **Publish**.

**What you'll see on screen:** A visual flowchart with blocks connected by arrows. Each block represents an action — SMS, email, wait, condition. The system runs it in the order you defined, 24/7, without any input from you.

## Step 3 — Set Up Online Scheduling (End the Back-and-Forth Messaging)

GoHighLevel's calendar lets clients **choose their own time slot** without you having to go back and forth over text to find something that works for both of you.

**How to set it up:**
1. Go to **Calendars → + Create Calendar**.
2. Choose the *Round Robin* type (if you have a team) or *Personal* (if it's just you for now).
3. Under **Availability**, set your working days and hours. Ex: Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
4. Under **Meeting Duration**, set 30 minutes (for a quote walkthrough or a service appointment).
5. Under **Buffer Time**, add 15 minutes between appointments.
6. Under **Confirmation Settings**, enable the **automatic SMS confirmation** right after booking and the **reminder SMS** 24 hours before.
7. Copy your calendar link — you'll paste it on your website and in your follow-up automation.

**What you'll see on screen:** A color-coded calendar with available slots shown in green. The client clicks a time, enters their name and phone number, and receives a confirmation — all without you touching your phone.

## Step 4 — Build Your Post-Quote Follow-Up Sequence

Most leads don't close on the first contact — but most cleaning business owners give up after the second message. An **automated follow-up sequence** handles this effortlessly.

**Recommended sequence for quotes:**

| Time After Quote Is Sent | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately | SMS | Confirmation that the quote was sent |
| 1 day | Email | Service benefits + client testimonials |
| 3 days | SMS | "Still interested? I can hold a spot for you" |
| 7 days | Email | Urgency offer or available slot this week |
| 14 days | SMS | Final contact attempt |

**How to set it up in GoHighLevel:**
1. Create a new Workflow with the **Trigger:** *Opportunity Stage Changed → Quote Sent*.
2. Add the SMS and Email blocks from the table above, with **Wait Steps** between each one.
3. Add a **Condition** after each message: *If replied → Remove from workflow* (so you don't keep messaging people who already responded).
4. At the end of the sequence, automatically move the lead to the *Cold Lead* stage in your pipeline.
5. Publish the workflow.

This sequence alone can recover between **20% and 35% of leads** you thought had gone cold.

## Step 5 — Automate Your Google Review Requests

An **automated review request** is a message sent automatically a few hours after a job is completed, asking the client to leave a Google review — while the experience is still fresh in their mind.

Cleaning businesses with more than 50 Google reviews receive up to **3x more profile clicks** than competitors with fewer than 10. Reviews are, in practice, free salespeople working around the clock.

**How to set it up:**
1. Create a new Workflow with the **Trigger:** *Appointment Status → Completed*.
2. Add a **Wait Step** of 3 hours (enough time for the client to get home and see the results).
3. Add a **Send SMS** with the message:
   > *"Hi [Name]! It was a pleasure cleaning your home today 😊 If you're happy with the results, I'd love it if you could leave a quick Google review — it takes less than a minute and means the world to us! [Google Business Profile link]"*
4. Add a **Wait Step** of 2 days and, if there's no response, send a follow-up email.
5. Publish.

**What you'll see on screen:** A simple workflow with 4 blocks. Every time an appointment is marked as completed, the system fires the sequence automatically — without you having to remember a thing.

## Step 6 — Win Back Inactive Clients (Reactivation Campaign)

Clients who used your service once and never came back are **forgotten gold** sitting in your database. An automated reactivation campaign can bring back 15% to 25% of those clients with zero acquisition cost.

**How to set it up:**
1. Go to **Automation → Workflows → + Create Workflow**.
2. Trigger: *Contact Tag Added → inactive-client* (you'll create this tag for contacts with no service in the last 60 days).
3. **Send SMS:**
   > *"Hey [Name], it's been a while since we've cleaned your home! We still have openings this week. Want to get back on the schedule? [calendar link]"*
4. Add a **Wait Step** of 5 days.
5. **Send Email** with a special offer for returning clients (ex: 10% off their next booking).
6. To automate the tagging, create a second workflow: Trigger *Last Activity → more than 60 days ago* → Action *Add Tag: inactive-client*.
