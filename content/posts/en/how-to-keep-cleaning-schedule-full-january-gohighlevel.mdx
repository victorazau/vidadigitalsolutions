## Step 1 — Segment Your Client Base in GoHighLevel

Before sending any messages, you need to know who you're talking to. In GoHighLevel, open the left-side menu and click **Contacts**.

**What you'll see on screen:** a full list of your contacts, filter fields at the top (Last Activity, Tags, Pipeline Stage), and a blue "Add Filter" button in the upper right corner.

Create three segments using the filters:

1. **Active clients** — Last Activity within the past 30 days. These are the clients you're currently serving during the holidays. They're the easiest to convert into January bookings.
2. **Inactive clients (60–120 days)** — stopped booking in the fall. They need a nudge to come back.
3. **Leads who never converted** — received a quote but never booked. January could be the right moment.

To save each segment, click **Save Segment** and give it a clear name (e.g., "Active Clients Nov 2026", "Inactive 60d", "Cold Leads 2026").

> **Pro tip:** If you use tags in GoHighLevel, add the tag "reactivate-january" to contacts in segments 2 and 3. This makes it easy to trigger targeted campaigns later.

## Step 2 — Create Your January Offer Before Christmas

You need a concrete reason to reach out. Without an offer, your message feels generic. With an offer, it converts.

Here are a few options that work well for cleaning businesses in the US:

- **Monthly package with a January discount:** "Book your January recurring cleaning this week and get 10% off the first month."
- **Rate lock-in:** "Lock in your 2026 rate before our January price adjustment."
- **Limited availability:** "We have 8 weekly slots for January — first come, first served."

In GoHighLevel, go to **Sites → Funnels** or **Sites → Websites** and build a simple landing page featuring your offer. You don't need anything elaborate — a headline, a short offer description, a form with name, phone, and address, and a submit button.

**What you'll see on screen:** GoHighLevel's drag-and-drop editor, with content blocks on the left (Headline, Image, Form, Button) and a live page preview on the right. Add a form block connected to your "January 2027 Recurring" pipeline.

## Step 3 — Build Your Automated Follow-Up Sequence

This is the core step. You'll create a **Workflow** in GoHighLevel that fires automatically when a contact enters your segment or campaign.

Go to **Automation → Workflows → + Create Workflow**. Choose **Start from Scratch**.

**Trigger:** select "Contact Tag Added" and choose the tag "reactivate-january". Any contact that receives this tag will automatically enter the sequence.

**Sequence structure (add actions in this order):**

### Day 0 — Initial SMS
Add the **Send SMS** action. Suggested message:

*"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, it's [Your Name] from [Business Name]! 🎄 We're booking January cleanings now — and we'd love to keep your home spotless in 2027. Reply YES and I'll hold a slot for you. Limited availability!"*

**What you'll see on screen:** a text field with dynamic variables available on the left ({{contact.first_name}}, {{contact.phone}}, etc.). Click any variable to insert it into your message.

### Day 2 — Follow-Up Email
Add a **Wait** step of 2 days, then a **Send Email** action. Use the email to go deeper on your package — before/after photos, client testimonials, and a link to the landing page you built in Step 2.

### Day 5 — Second SMS (Urgency)
*"Hi {{contact.first_name}}! Just checking in — we have 3 January slots left. Want me to hold one for you? Reply YES or call us at [phone number]."*

### Day 8 — Manual Task (Phone Call)
Add a **Create Task** action with the description "Call {{contact.first_name}} — final January follow-up". This appears in your team's dashboard as a pending task.

> **Tip:** Add an "If/Else" condition before each message to check whether the contact has already replied ("Last Reply" field). If they have, remove them from the sequence and move them to your scheduling pipeline. GoHighLevel handles this natively using the **Go To** action and branch conditions.

## Step 4 — Set Up a Mass SMS Reactivation Campaign

In addition to the automated sequence, you can send a one-time broadcast to your entire inactive client list.

In GoHighLevel, go to **Marketing → Campaigns → + New Campaign**.

**What you'll see on screen:** a creation wizard with four tabs at the top — Recipients, Message, Schedule, and Review. Work through each tab:

1. **Recipients:** select the saved segment "Inactive 60d"
2. **Message:** write your reactivation message (use the same Day 0 text from above, lightly adapted)
3. **Schedule:** pick a Tuesday or Wednesday between 10 AM and 2 PM in your local market's time zone. These windows consistently produce the highest SMS open rates
4. **Review:** confirm the estimated recipient count and click **Send Campaign**

> **Important:** In the US, SMS marketing regulations (TCPA and A2P 10DLC rules) require that contacts have given explicit opt-in consent to receive marketing messages. Make sure your contact list has a documented consent history. GoHighLevel includes built-in opt-in and compliance fields to help with this.

## Step 5 — Turn Every Holiday Job Into a Recurring Contract on the Spot

This is the highest-ROI tactic of all — and it doesn't require any complex automation.

Right after a holiday cleaning (or shortly after, via an automated post-service SMS), make your recurring offer. In GoHighLevel, create a **Workflow with the trigger "Appointment Status Changed to Completed"**:

- **Immediately:** an automated satisfaction SMS — *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, hope your home is sparkling! 🌟 How was today's cleaning? Reply 1–5."*
- **30 minutes later:** an offer SMS — *"Glad you loved it! 🎉 Want to lock in a recurring schedule for January? We're offering 10% off for clients who book this week. Reply YES!"*

**What you'll see on screen when setting up the trigger:** a list of available events in GoHighLevel. Choose "Appointment" and filter by status "Completed". Add both SMS actions with the 30-minute wait between them.

This sequence works while you're at the next house cleaning. Satisfied clients reached at the right moment convert at rates of 25–40%, based on data from GoHighLevel operators in the home services industry.
