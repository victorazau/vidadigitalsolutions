## GoHighLevel Reputation Management: How It Works in Practice

GoHighLevel's Reputation Management module works as an automated trigger system connected directly to your sales pipeline or service schedule. When a job is marked as completed — whether it's a home cleaning, a legal consultation, or any other service — GoHighLevel automatically fires off a sequence to that customer.

### The Core Automation Flow

1. **Trigger:** Opportunity moved to "Service Completed" stage in the pipeline
2. **Wait:** 2–4 hours (the sweet spot when the experience is still fresh in the customer's mind)
3. **Automated SMS:** Personalized message with a direct link to your Google Review page
4. **Fallback:** If the SMS isn't opened within 24 hours, an email fires with the same link
5. **Second chance:** If no action is taken, a soft reminder SMS goes out at the 3-day mark

The link sent is a **direct link to your Google Business Profile review page** — the customer taps it, Google Maps opens, and they're already on the review screen. No friction, no searching for your business name.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Reputation Management in GoHighLevel

### 1. Connect Your Google Business Profile

Go to **Settings → Integrations → Google Business Profile** in your GHL dashboard. Sign in with the Google account that manages your profile and authorize the integration. Once connected, GHL can read and respond to reviews directly.

### 2. Build the Review Request Workflow

Navigate to **Automation → Workflows → New Workflow**. Configure the following:
- **Trigger:** Pipeline Stage Changed → Stage: [Service Completed]
- **Action 1:** Wait — 3 hours
- **Action 2:** Send SMS — Use your review request template with the review link
- **Action 3:** Wait — 24 hours
- **Action 4:** If/Else — Was the SMS opened? No → Send Email
- **Action 5:** Wait — 72 hours
- **Action 6:** Send SMS (optional final reminder)

For a more detailed workflow setup walkthrough, see our [Complete GoHighLevel Workflow Builder Guide](/blog/gohighlevel-workflow-builder-complete-guide-2026).

### 3. Write SMS Templates That Actually Convert

Your template makes all the difference. Skip the stiff corporate language. Something like this works well:

> "Hey {{contact.first_name}}! It was great working with you. If you're happy with our service, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review — it takes less than a minute: {{review.link}} Thank you! — [Business Name]"

Short, personal messages with a single clear call-to-action consistently outperform long, formal ones.

### 4. Enable the Review Widget on Your Website

GHL automatically generates a widget that displays your Google reviews on your website. Enable it under **Reputation → Settings → Review Widget** and paste the embed code into your site. Displaying reviews on your site builds visitor trust and strengthens your local SEO.

### 5. Build a Response Routine

Set up a GHL notification so you're alerted every time a new review comes in. Respond to all reviews — positive and negative — within 24 hours. Google treats your response rate as an engagement signal, which factors into your Maps ranking.
