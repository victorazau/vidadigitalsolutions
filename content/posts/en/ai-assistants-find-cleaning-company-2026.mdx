## How Do AI Assistants Decide Which Cleaning Company to Recommend?

AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity don't work like Google. They don't show a list of 10 results — they give **one answer**, sometimes two or three options. That changes everything.

When someone asks *"find a reliable cleaning company in Orlando,"* the AI pulls from a combination of sources:

1. **Google Business Profile data** (name, reviews, location, categories)
2. **Your website content** (clear language, Q&A sections, services listed with detail)
3. **Google and Yelp reviews** (quantity, quality, and frequency)
4. **External mentions** (directories, blog articles, local press)

AI prioritizes businesses that appear **trustworthy, well-described, and well-reviewed**. There's no magic formula — but there is a clear pattern: businesses with a complete Google Business Profile, 40+ recent reviews, and a website that answers common questions have a massive advantage.

One important data point: industry research shows that **more than 60% of local searches in 2026 already result in zero clicks on Google** — users get the answer directly on their screen. For cleaning companies, this means ranking on Google's organic results is no longer enough on its own.

## What to Change on Your Website Right Now to Show Up in AI Search

AI assistants "read" your website differently than a human visitor. They look for **objective, self-contained answers**. Here's what works:

### 1. Write Direct Answers to Common Questions

Instead of *"Our company offers the best residential cleaning services,"* write:

*"We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly residential cleaning in [city]. We serve homes with 1 to 5 bedrooms. Average pricing ranges from $120 to $280 per cleaning, depending on the size of the home."*

AI can extract that information and cite it as a direct answer when a user asks about pricing or availability.

### 2. Add a FAQ Section to Your Website

Questions like:
- *"Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?"*
- *"How do I book a cleaning online?"*
- *"Are your cleaners background checked?"*

Are exactly what people ask AI before hiring a cleaning service. If your website answers those questions clearly, you significantly increase your chances of being cited.

### 3. Mention Your City and Service Area Explicitly

Don't just write "serving the area" — write "serving Miami, Doral, Hialeah, and surrounding communities." AI uses specific geographic entities to connect your business with local searches.

### 4. Update Your Google Business Profile Every Week

Post new photos, respond to reviews, and add new services. AI platforms use GBP data as a trusted source because it's verified by Google. A consistently updated profile signals that the business is active.

## Reviews: The Most Underrated Factor in AI Visibility

If there's one thing that separates cleaning companies that show up in AI from those that don't, it's **reviews**. And it's not just about the star rating — it's about **frequency and content**.

Here's what AI platforms value in reviews:

- **Quantity:** Businesses with 50+ reviews appear more often than businesses with 10, even if the ratings are similar.
- **Recency:** Reviews from the past 90 days carry more weight than older ones. A business with 20 new reviews can outrank one with 80 old reviews.
- **Keywords in reviews:** When customers mention "deep cleaning," "move-out," or "Airbnb cleaning" in their reviews, AI associates your business with those specific services.
- **Responses to reviews:** Businesses that reply to reviews signal that the company is active and engaged.

**Q: Does AI actually recommend local cleaning companies?**
**A:** Yes. Recommendations are based primarily on Google Business Profile, reviews, and website content.

**Q: Do I need to completely redo my website to show up in AI?**
**A:** You don't need to start over. The most impactful changes are: adding a FAQ section with direct answers, explicitly mentioning your city and services, and making sure your GBP is complete and regularly updated. These adjustments alone can significantly increase your AI visibility.

**Q: How many Google reviews does my cleaning company need to show up in AI?**
**A:** There's no official minimum, but businesses with 40 or more recent reviews (from the past 6 months) appear far more frequently in AI responses than businesses with fewer reviews — even if the average star rating is similar. For maximum visibility, you need both.
