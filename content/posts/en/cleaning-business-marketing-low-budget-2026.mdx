## Why Google Business Profile Is Still King in 2026

Google Business Profile (GBP) is the free listing that appears on Google Maps and in local search results when someone searches "house cleaning [your city]" or "cleaning service near me." And in 2026, that search is still the primary entry point for new cleaning clients.

Industry data shows that **64% of searches with local intent result in an action** — a call, a message, or a visit. For a cleaning business, that means anyone who finds your profile on Maps is already ready to hire someone. The only question is whether it'll be you or your competitor.

**What your GBP needs to rank at the top:**
- Real photos of your work (before and after, at least 10 photos)
- A description listing the cities and neighborhoods you serve
- Up-to-date business hours
- Responses to all customer questions
- A steady flow of reviews (at least 1 per week)

Beyond traditional Google search, AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews also pull data from GBP to recommend local businesses. So optimizing your profile isn't just about SEO anymore — it's about making sure AI recommends you when someone asks *"what's the best cleaning company in [your city]?"*

## Instagram and Social Media: The Right Role, The Right Expectations

Instagram isn't bad for cleaning companies — but it does one specific thing: **it builds familiarity and trust** with people who already know you or follow you. It rarely brings in a brand-new client from scratch.

What actually works on Instagram for cleaning businesses:
- Before-and-after videos (this format gets strong organic reach)
- Behind-the-scenes content showing your team at work (it humanizes your brand)
- Video testimonials from happy clients
- Quick cleaning and organization tips

What doesn't work:
- Only posting prices and promotions
- Inconsistent Stories
- Ignoring comments and DMs

**Practical rule:** Spend 80% of your marketing time on Google Business Profile and automation. Use the remaining 20% on Instagram. Don't flip that ratio.
