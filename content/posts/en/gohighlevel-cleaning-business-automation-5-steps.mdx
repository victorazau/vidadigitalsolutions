## Step 1 — Set Up Your Sales Pipeline for Cleaning

The pipeline is the heart of GoHighLevel. It shows you exactly where each lead stands in your sales process — from first contact all the way to a recurring client paying every month.

**What you'll see on screen:** Once you're logged into GoHighLevel, click **CRM** in the left sidebar. Then click **Pipelines** and select **+ Add Pipeline**. Name it something like "Cleaning Clients."

**Create the following stages:**

1. **New Lead** — came in through your website, Google, or a referral
2. **Quote Sent** — you've already sent them pricing
3. **Negotiating** — client responded and is considering
4. **Deal Closed** — client confirmed the service
5. **Service Scheduled** — date and time are locked in
6. **Recurring Client** — booked a second cleaning or signed a monthly contract

**Pro tip:** Don't overcomplicate it with too many stages. A simple pipeline is easier to manage and easier to train any future staff on.

Click **Save** and your pipeline is ready. From now on, every new lead will start in the "New Lead" column, and you'll move them visually as the relationship progresses.

## Step 2 — Set Up Automatic Lead Capture

A great pipeline means nothing if leads aren't flowing into it automatically. In this step, you'll connect your lead sources to GoHighLevel.

**What you'll see on screen:** In the left sidebar, go to **Sites** → **Forms** → **+ New Form**. Choose the **Simple Contact Form** template.

For cleaning businesses, we recommend the following fields:
- Full name
- Phone number (WhatsApp)
- Email
- City / ZIP code of the property
- Type of cleaning (dropdown: House Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-in/Move-out, Airbnb)
- Desired frequency (dropdown: One-time, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly)

After saving the form, go to **Settings → Integrations** and connect:
- **Google Business Profile** — so leads who click "Contact Us" on Google Maps go straight into GoHighLevel
- **Facebook Lead Ads** — if you run ads on Facebook or Instagram
- **Twilio** — to send and receive SMS automatically (essential for Step 3)

## Step 3 — Build Your Automatic Follow-Up Workflow

This is the most important step. The workflow is the automation that works for you while you're out on the job.

**What you'll see on screen:** In the left sidebar, click **Automation** → **Workflows** → **+ Create Workflow**. Select **Start from Scratch**.

**Trigger:** Select **Form Submitted** and choose the form you created in Step 2.

**Actions to configure in sequence:**

**Action 1 — Instant SMS (within 60 seconds):**
- Click **+ Add Action** → **Send SMS**
- Suggested message: *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}! This is [Business Name]. We received your cleaning request and will get back to you in the next few minutes. Questions? Reply here anytime!"*

**Action 2 — Add to Pipeline:**
- Click **+ Add Action** → **Create Opportunity**
- Pipeline: "Cleaning Clients" | Stage: "New Lead"

**Action 3 — Internal notification for you:**
- Click **+ Add Action** → **Internal Notification**
- Message: *"New lead: {{contact.full_name}} — {{contact.phone}}. Type: {{cleaning_type}}"*

**Action 4 — Follow-up email (1 hour later):**
- Click **+ Add Action** → **Wait** → 1 hour
- Then **Send Email** with more details about your services, photos of past work, and a link to schedule

**Action 5 — Follow-up SMS (24 hours later, if no reply):**
- Wait → 24 hours
- **Condition:** check if the lead has replied (if yes, stop; if no, send SMS)
- SMS: *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}, just checking in! We'd love to give you a free quote for your cleaning. Reply here or call us anytime."*

Click **Save & Publish**. Now any lead who fills out your form gets a response in under 60 seconds — even if you're in the middle of a job.

## Step 4 — Set Up Online Scheduling

Many cleaning clients prefer to book from their phone without having to call or go back and forth in messages. GoHighLevel has a built-in calendar system that eliminates the scheduling back-and-forth entirely.

**What you'll see on screen:** In the left sidebar, click **Calendars** → **+ Create Calendar**. Choose **Round Robin** (if you have multiple cleaners) or **Personal Calendar** (if it's just you).

**Configure the following:**
- Service name: e.g., "Schedule a Free Assessment"
- Duration: 15–30 minutes (for a quote call) or 2–4 hours (for the cleaning itself)
- Availability: your working days and hours
- Buffer time: 30 minutes between appointments (for travel time)

**Integrate with your workflow:** Go back to the workflow from Step 3 and add a button with your calendar link to the follow-up email. Example: *"Book your free assessment here: [link]."*

**Pro tip:** Enable automatic SMS and email confirmations — GoHighLevel sends reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before each appointment, reducing no-shows by up to 60%.

## Step 5 — Automate Your Google Review Requests

Google reviews are the most valuable asset a cleaning business has. When someone searches "house cleaning near me," they compare star ratings before anything else. GoHighLevel automates the review request right after a job is completed.

**What you'll see on screen:** Go to **Reputation** → **Request Review** → configure your message template.

**Create a second workflow specifically for reviews:**
- **Trigger:** Opportunity Stage Changed → "Service Completed"
- **Wait:** 2 hours (enough time for the client to get home and enjoy their clean space)
- **Send SMS:** *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}! Hope you're loving your clean home! 🏠 Would you mind leaving us a Google review? It takes 30 seconds and means the world to us: [Google link]"*
- **Wait:** 3 days (if no review left)
- **Send Email:** a more detailed email with the same request

**Connect your Google Business Profile:** Go to **Settings → Integrations → Google My Business** and sign in with your business Google account. Your review link will be generated automatically.

**Expected results:** Cleaning businesses that implement this automation report 3x to 5x more Google reviews within 60 days — simply because the request goes out at the right moment, in a friendly tone, with a direct link. No more trying to remember to ask manually.
