## Step 1 — Set Up the "One-Time Client" Tag in GoHighLevel

Everything starts with organizing your contacts. In GoHighLevel, every client is a contact, and you can label them with **tags** to automatically segment them.

**What you'll see on screen:** Go to the left-side menu and click **Contacts**. Select a contact and, in the right panel, find the **Tags** field. Click the pencil icon and type `one-time-client`. Save.

**How to do this automatically for every new client:**

1. In the left menu, go to **Automation → Workflows**
2. Click **+ Create Workflow**
3. Select **Start from Scratch**
4. For the trigger, choose **Appointment Status Changed**
5. Set the status to **Showed** (service completed)
6. Add the action **Add Tag** → `one-time-client`
7. Click **Save & Publish**

From this point on, every client whose appointment is marked as completed will receive the tag automatically — no manual work needed on your end.

## Step 2 — Build Your Post-Cleaning Follow-Up Sequence

This is the heart of the system. The follow-up sequence is a series of automatic messages sent at the right intervals after the first cleaning to convert one-time clients into recurring ones.

**Recommended structure (tested by Vida Digital Solutions with US-based cleaning businesses):**

| Day | Channel | Message |
|-----|---------|----------|
| Day 0 (immediately after) | SMS | Thank-you message + Google review link |
| Day 2 | Email | "How does your home look?" + satisfaction check-in |
| Day 7 | SMS | Monthly plan offer with a discount |
| Day 14 | Email | Benefits of a recurring contract + client testimonial |
| Day 21 | SMS | Final reminder + soft urgency ("October schedule filling up fast") |

**How to set this up in GoHighLevel:**

1. Open the Workflow you created in Step 1
2. After the **Add Tag** action, click **+** to add a new action
3. Choose **Send SMS** — write your thank-you message (max 160 characters)
4. Click **+** again → select **Wait** → set it to **2 days**
5. Add **Send Email** — write your satisfaction check-in email (see template below)
6. Repeat the process for days 7, 14, and 21

**SMS Template for Day 0:**
> "Hi [Name]! Thank you for choosing [Your Business]. We hope your home is spotless! 😊 Would you mind leaving us a quick review? [Google link]"

**SMS Template for Day 7:**
> "Hi [Name]! Want to keep your home consistently clean? Our monthly plan is just $X/month — less than a one-time clean. Can I get you scheduled? Reply YES!"

## Step 3 — Set Up Your Recurring Contract Proposal

When a client replies "YES" or clicks your offer link, they need to automatically receive a clear proposal and a payment link. No phone calls, no manual messages.

**What you'll see on screen:** In GoHighLevel, go to **Payments → Products** and create a product called "Monthly Cleaning Plan". Set the price, frequency (monthly), and enable the **recurring billing** option.

**Step-by-step setup:**

1. Go to **Payments → Products → + Add Product**
2. Name: "Monthly Plan — Residential Cleaning"
3. Price: set your rate (e.g., $180/month for a standard home)
4. Type: **Recurring** → frequency **Monthly**
5. Save the product
6. Go to **Payments → Payment Links** → create a link tied to that product
7. Copy the generated link

Now go back to your Workflow and add that link to the Day 7 SMS and the Day 14 email. When a client clicks and pays, GoHighLevel logs it automatically — and you can trigger a new tag: `recurring-client`.

**Practical tip:** Create an introductory offer — for example, 10% off the first month for clients who sign up before the end of September. This creates real urgency without coming across as pushy.

## Step 4 — Automate Your Google Review Requests

Happy clients rarely leave reviews on their own — but when you ask at the right moment, response rates can reach 25%. And Google reviews are the number one decision factor for new clients searching for cleaning services in your area.

**How to set up the automated review request:**

1. In GoHighLevel, go to **Reputation → Settings**
2. Connect your Google Business Profile to your account
3. Go back to your Workflow → add a **Send Review Request** action right after the Day 0 thank-you SMS
4. GoHighLevel automatically sends an SMS with a direct link to your Google review page

**What your client receives:** A simple text message with a direct link to your Google Maps page. With two taps, they leave a review. No app to download, no login required.

## Step 5 — Build Your Recurring Client Pipeline

Now that the system is running, you need visibility. A pipeline in GoHighLevel works like a visual board where you can see exactly what stage each client is at — think of it as a Kanban board for your business.

**How to create the pipeline:**

1. Go to **CRM → Pipelines → + Add Pipeline**
2. Name it "Cleaning Clients — Retention"
3. Create the following stages (columns):
   - **New One-Time Client**
   - **Follow-Up Sent**
   - **Plan Proposal Sent**
   - **Proposal Accepted — Awaiting Payment**
   - **Active Recurring Client**
   - **Inactive (Re-engage)**
4. Configure your Workflow to automatically move contacts between stages as actions are completed

**What you'll see on screen:** A color-coded board with contact cards in each column. At a glance, you can see how many clients are at each stage — and focus your energy where it makes the biggest difference.
