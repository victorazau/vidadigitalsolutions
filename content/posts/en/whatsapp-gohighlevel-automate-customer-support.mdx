## How to Set Up WhatsApp in GoHighLevel: A Simplified Step-by-Step

For those who want to understand the process before diving in:

**1. Create a Meta Business Suite Account**
You'll need a verified Meta Business Manager account and a dedicated phone number for WhatsApp Business — it cannot be the same number as your personal WhatsApp.

**2. Create an App in Meta for Developers**
Go to developers.facebook.com, create a "Business" type app, enable the WhatsApp product, and copy your Permanent Access Token and Phone Number ID.

**3. Connect to GoHighLevel**
In your GHL dashboard, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**. Paste your Token, Phone Number ID, and Webhook Verify Token. Save and validate the connection.

**4. Configure Your Number**
Verify the number through Meta and wait for approval — typically 24 to 48 hours — then set your business display name.

**5. Build Your First Workflows**
In GoHighLevel, create automations using the "Incoming WhatsApp Message" trigger and define your responses, conditions, and actions. Start simple: an automatic response for after-hours messages alone is already a massive win.

**6. Test and Monitor**
Send test messages, confirm that your workflows fire correctly, and refine your copy as needed.

> ⚠️ Important: The WhatsApp Business API enforces strict content policies. Bulk marketing messages require pre-approved templates from Meta. Use the platform responsibly to avoid getting your number banned.

**A:** Yes, you'll need a phone number that isn't already associated with a personal WhatsApp account. This can be a VoIP number or a dedicated business line. The number will be registered as a WhatsApp Business API account and linked to your Meta Business Manager.

**Q: Can GoHighLevel send bulk messages through WhatsApp?**
**A:** Yes, but with important restrictions from Meta. Broadcast messages can only be sent to contacts who have interacted with your number within the last 24 hours, or by using pre-approved message templates from Meta.
