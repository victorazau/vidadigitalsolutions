## What Franchises Do (And How You Can Replicate It)

Let's demystify what the big cleaning chains actually do differently. It's not magic — it's process:

**1. Instant response:** Every lead gets a reply within minutes, regardless of the time of day.

**2. Automatic follow-up:** If a client didn't book on first contact, they receive a sequence of follow-up messages over the next several days.

**3. Frictionless scheduling:** Clients pick their date and time online — no phone tag, no waiting for confirmation.

**4. Automatic reminders:** 24 hours before the service, the client receives a confirmation. This reduces no-shows by up to 60%.

**5. Review requests:** A few hours after the service, the client gets an SMS asking for a Google review.

## Missed-Call Text-Back: The Simplest and Most Powerful Automation

Missed-call text-back is an automation that sends an automatic SMS whenever you miss a phone call. The message might look something like this:

*"Hi! This is Maria from Maria's Cleaning. I saw you called — my hands are tied up right now but I'd love to help. What do you need cleaned, and what day works best for you?"*

This simple message does three things at once:
1. Shows the client you're professional and attentive
2. Keeps them in your pipeline instead of calling a competitor
3. Collects information so you're already prepared when you follow up

On average, cleaning businesses that implement this feature report **35% more booked quotes** with zero changes to their pricing or service.
