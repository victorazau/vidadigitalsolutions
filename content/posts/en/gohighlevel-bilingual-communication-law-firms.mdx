## Step 1 — Create a Custom Language Field

The first step is creating a way to identify each contact's preferred language inside GoHighLevel.

**In your GHL dashboard:**
1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon, bottom-left corner)
2. Click **Custom Fields**
3. Click **+ Add Field**
4. Configure it as follows:
   - **Field Label:** Preferred Language
   - **Field Type:** Dropdown
   - **Options:** Portuguese / Spanish / English
5. Click **Save**

**What you'll see on screen:** A new row will appear in your custom fields list labeled "Preferred Language" with the type "Dropdown." This field will be available across all forms, workflows, and contact records in the system.

> **Pro tip:** You can also use this field as a filter in Smart Lists to segment your contact base and send separate email or SMS campaigns by language.

## Step 2 — Set Up Bilingual Lead Capture Forms

Now you need the lead's language preference to be captured automatically the moment they enter your system. There are two approaches:

### Approach A: Single Form with a Language Field

1. Go to **Sites → Forms → + New Form**
2. Drag in the fields: Name, Phone, Email
3. Also drag in the **Preferred Language** field (created in Step 1)
4. Set the language field as **Required**
5. Save and publish the form

**What the lead will see:** A clean form with a straightforward question — "What language do you prefer?" — with three dropdown options. Simple and effective.

### Approach B: Separate Forms per Language

Alternatively, create three separate landing pages — one in Portuguese, one in Spanish, one in English — each with its own form. In this case:

1. In each form, add a **Hidden Field** mapped to "Preferred Language"
2. Set the default hidden value to "Portuguese," "Spanish," or "English" based on the page
3. The lead doesn't need to choose — the system detects their language from the entry point

This approach works especially well for targeted ad campaigns: run a Portuguese-language campaign aimed at Brazilian communities and a Spanish one for Hispanic audiences, each pointing to the landing page in the correct language.

## Step 3 — Create Language Tags with Automation

Tags are the fastest way to segment contacts and trigger workflows in GoHighLevel. Let's build a simple automation that applies a tag based on the language the lead selected.

1. Go to **Automation → Workflows → + New Workflow**
2. Name it: **[TAG] Set Contact Language**
3. **Trigger:** Form Submitted → select the form created in Step 2
4. Add an **If/Else** action:
   - **Condition 1:** "Preferred Language" field equals "Portuguese" → Add Tag: `lang-pt`
   - **Condition 2:** "Preferred Language" field equals "Spanish" → Add Tag: `lang-es`
   - **Else (default):** Add Tag: `lang-en`
5. Activate the workflow and save

**What happens in practice:** From this point forward, every lead who submits the form will automatically receive a language tag. This tag is what triggers the bilingual response workflows in the next steps.

## Step 4 — Build Language-Specific Response Workflows

This is the core step. You'll create separate workflows for each language, each with its own customized messages.

### Portuguese Workflow

1. **New Workflow** → Name: **Auto Response — Portuguese**
2. **Trigger:** Tag Added → Tag: `lang-pt`
3. Add the following actions in sequence:

| Timing | Action | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediately | Send SMS | SMS | "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, we received your message. One of our attorneys will be in touch shortly. Stay tuned!" (in Portuguese) |
| Immediately | Send Email | Email | Welcome email in Portuguese with next steps |
| +1 hour | Send SMS | SMS | "{{contact.first_name}}, would you like to schedule a consultation? Click here: {{appointment.link}}" (in Portuguese) |
| +24 hours | Send Email | Email | Follow-up email in Portuguese highlighting practice areas |

4. Repeat the same structure for **Spanish** (tag `lang-es`) and **English** (tag `lang-en`)

> **Pro tip:** Use GoHighLevel's **Templates** feature to save each message. That way you can reuse them across email, SMS, and WhatsApp channels without rewriting from scratch.

## Step 5 — Configure WhatsApp Messaging by Language

WhatsApp is the preferred communication channel for Brazilian and Latino clients in the US. Setting up automated messages in Portuguese and Spanish via WhatsApp is a significant competitive advantage for firms serving these communities.

**Prerequisite:** An active WhatsApp Business integration in GHL (via Meta Business Manager).

1. Inside the workflow from Step 4, after the first SMS action, add a **Send WhatsApp Message** action
2. Select the Meta-approved template in the corresponding language
3. Personalize with variables: `{{contact.first_name}}`, `{{user.name}}` (assigned attorney)

**Example WhatsApp message in Portuguese:**
> "Hi, *{{contact.first_name}}*! 👋 This is [Firm Name]. We received your message and are already reviewing your case. One of our attorneys will reach out within 2 hours. If it's urgent, just reply here."

**What the client will experience:** A formatted WhatsApp message in their own language, arriving within minutes of submitting the form. That immediate, personalized response creates a powerful first impression of professionalism and care — setting your firm apart from competitors.

## Step 6 — Test and Validate Every Flow

Before going live with real clients, run thorough tests across every language path:

1. **Create three test contacts** in GHL — one for each language
2. **Manually submit the form** for each test contact
3. Verify that:
   - The correct tag was applied (check under **Contacts → contact → Tags**)
   - The SMS arrived at the test number in the correct language
   - The email was sent using the correct template
   - The WhatsApp message fired (if configured)
4. **Open the workflow** and review the **History** for each execution — GHL shows a step-by-step breakdown of what ran and whether any errors occurred

**What you'll see in History:** A timeline of every action executed, including status (Success / Failed), timestamp, and the content that was sent. If something fails, GHL pinpoints exactly which step caused the issue.

> **Pro tip:** Set up a **Smart List** called "Leads by Language" filtered by tag. This lets any team member quickly see how many leads came in for each language and track the status of each one at a glance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does GoHighLevel properly support Portuguese and Spanish characters like accents and special letters?**
**A:** Yes. GoHighLevel supports full Unicode, which includes accented characters, the cedilla (ç), and all special characters used in Brazilian Portuguese and Spanish. Emails, SMS messages, and WhatsApp messages are all sent correctly with these characters. One thing to note: SMS in GSM-7 format supports up to 160 characters, but Unicode messages are limited to 70 characters per segment — GHL automatically splits longer messages into multiple segments when needed.

**Q: Do I need a separate GoHighLevel account for each language?**
**A:** No. The entire bilingual (or trilingual) setup lives inside a single GoHighLevel sub-account. You use custom fields, tags, and workflows to separate the language flows without duplicating accounts. This keeps management simple and all your data centralized in one dashboard.

**Q: Can the attorney also receive an internal notification that indicates the client's language?**
**A:** Yes. Inside the workflow, you can add an **Internal Notification** action that sends an email or SMS to the assigned attorney with the client's language flagged. For example: "New lead — follow up in PORTUGUESE. Name: Maria Costa. Phone: (305) 555-0000." This prepares the attorney before they make human contact.
