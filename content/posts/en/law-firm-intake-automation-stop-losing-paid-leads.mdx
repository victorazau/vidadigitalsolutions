## What Is Legal Intake Automation (Definition)

Legal intake automation is a system that captures a prospect's information the moment they submit a form or call your office — and immediately triggers a sequence of communications (SMS, email, and optionally WhatsApp) to qualify them, nurture them, and guide them to a booked consultation, without any manual effort from your team.

The system runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. While your attorneys are in court, sleeping, or enjoying the weekend, your pipeline keeps moving.

The core components of a robust automated intake system are:

1. **Smart capture form** — integrated with your website, Google Ads campaign, or landing page
2. **Immediate welcome SMS** — sent within 2 minutes of form submission
3. **Automatic qualification** — a short sequence of questions to determine whether the case fits your practice areas
4. **Automated scheduling** — a calendar booking link sent to qualified leads so they can book a consultation without speaking to anyone
5. **Nurturing sequence** — email and SMS reminders leading up to the consultation, reducing no-shows
6. **Internal notification** — the attorney or paralegal receives a pre-qualified lead summary before the meeting

## How to Implement: The 4 Layers of Automated Intake

### Layer 1 — Instant Capture

The moment a lead submits your form or taps "Call Now" on Google, the system creates a contact in your CRM and fires the first SMS within 2 minutes. The message is personalized with the lead's name and references the specific case type they indicated in the form.

Real message example: *"Hi [Name], we received your request regarding your [practice area] case. One of our specialists will review your submission shortly. In the meantime, you can schedule your free consultation here: [link]."*

### Layer 2 — Automatic Qualification

Not every lead who clicks your ad is a viable client. The system sends a sequence of 2–3 questions via SMS or email to identify: case urgency, location, general budget range, and specific legal need.

Leads who qualify are automatically moved to the scheduling stage. Leads outside your target profile receive a polite response with a referral to another resource — without consuming a single minute of any attorney's time.

### Layer 3 — Friction-Free Scheduling

The booking link goes directly to the responsible attorney's calendar, with pre-configured availability slots. The client selects their preferred time, confirms via SMS, and receives automatic reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the consultation.

This layer alone reduces no-shows by an average of **35%** — because the client has actively committed to a time slot and receives personalized reminders.

### Layer 4 — Nurturing Through to Close

For qualified leads who haven't booked yet, the system maintains contact for up to 14 days through a cadence of emails and texts that educate, build trust, and create contextual urgency. None of these messages feel like spam — they're personalized with the prospect's name, case type, and current stage in the funnel.
