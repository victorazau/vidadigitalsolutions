## 1. Understand What Reputation Management Is in GoHighLevel

**Reputation Management** is a native GoHighLevel module that lets you manage your business's online reputation from a single place. It includes three core features:

- **Automatic review requests:** Send SMS and/or email asking clients to leave a review on Google (or other platforms).
- **Review monitoring:** See all incoming reviews directly in your dashboard — no need to open Google separately.
- **Review responses:** Reply to positive and negative reviews directly from GoHighLevel, including AI-suggested responses.

To access the module, log in to your GoHighLevel account, select your firm's sub-account, and click **"Reputation"** in the left-side menu. You'll see a screen with three tabs: *Overview*, *Reviews*, and *Requests*.

> **What you'll see on screen:** A dashboard showing your current average Google rating, total review count, and two main buttons — "Send Request" (manual) and "Automations" (automatic). The second option is what we're going to set up.

## 2. Set Up Your Reputation Profile and Connect Your Google Business Profile

Before building any automation, you need to connect your firm's Google Business Profile to GoHighLevel. Without this connection, the platform can't pull in your existing reviews or direct clients to the right place.

**How to do it:**

1. In the left-side menu, go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Scroll down to find **Google My Business** and click **Connect**.
3. Sign in with the Google account that manages your Google Business Profile.
4. Select your firm's profile from the list and click **Connect Location**.

> **What you'll see on screen:** After connecting, a Google icon will turn green with your firm's name next to it. If you see an error, verify that you're listed as an admin in your Google Business Profile Manager.

**Practical tip:** If your firm serves specific cultural communities or multilingual clients, make sure your Google Business Profile name matches exactly what your clients recognize — this increases recognition rates when the review request lands in their inbox.

Once connected, go back to the **Reputation** module and open **Settings**. Here you'll define:

- **Review link:** GoHighLevel automatically fills in the direct link to your Google review page.
- **Additional platforms:** You can add links to Facebook or other directories relevant to your practice area.
- **Send threshold:** Set it to request a review only once per contact (recommended).

## 3. Build the Automatic Review Request Workflow

This is the core step of the tutorial. We're going to create a **Workflow** in GoHighLevel that automatically triggers a review request after a specific event — for example, when a consultation is marked as completed.

If you're new to GoHighLevel's automation builder, we recommend reading the [complete GoHighLevel Workflow Builder guide](/blog/gohighlevel-workflow-builder-complete-guide-2026) before continuing.

**Step by step:**

1. In the left-side menu, go to **Automation → Workflows** and click **+ Create Workflow**.
2. Choose **Start from Scratch** and give it a clear name: *"Review Request — Post Consultation"*.
3. Click **Add New Trigger** and select the appropriate trigger. For law firms, the most common options are:
   - **Appointment Status Changed** → Status: *Showed* (client attended the consultation)
   - **Pipeline Stage Changed** → Stage: *Case Closed* (if you use a case management pipeline)
   - **Tag Added** → Tag: *consultation-completed* (if you manually tag contacts after each appointment)
4. Click **Save Trigger**.

> **What you'll see on screen:** The trigger appears as a blue block at the top of the flow. Below it, a **"+"** button lets you add the next actions.

**Adding a delay:**

Don't send the review request immediately — wait 1 to 2 hours so the client has had time to leave the appointment and is in a more relaxed mindset.

5. Click **"+"** and select **Wait** → **Wait for a specific time** → set it to **1 hour**.

**Adding the Review Request action:**

6. Click **"+"** again and search for **Send Review Request** in the action list.
7. Select your delivery channel: **SMS**, **Email**, or **both**. For law firms, the SMS + email combination consistently delivers the highest review conversion rates.
8. GoHighLevel includes a default template. You can personalize it using the variable `{{contact.first_name}}` to include the client's name.

**Example SMS message:**
> *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}! It was a pleasure working with you today. If you have a quick minute, your Google review helps our firm help more people like you: {{review_link}}"*

9. Click **Save Action**, then click **Publish** in the top-right corner to activate the workflow.

## 4. Customize Your SMS and Email Templates

The conversion rate of your review requests depends directly on the quality of the message. A generic message like "Please rate our service" performs far worse than a personalized, context-aware one.

**Best practices for law firms:**

| Element | What to do | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Warm and personal | Corporate and cold |
| Length | SMS: up to 160 characters | Long-winded messages |
| Link | Direct to the Google review form | Generic website link |
| Send time | Between 10 AM and 6 PM (local time) | After 8 PM or before 9 AM |
| Language | Match the client's preferred language | Wrong language for the contact |

For bilingual firms serving Spanish-speaking or Portuguese-speaking clients in the US, Vida Digital Solutions recommends creating **two separate workflows** — one in English and one in the client's preferred language — triggered by language tags on the contact record. This significantly increases response rates.

To configure the review request email, go to **Reputation → Settings → Review Request Email** and edit the template using the visual editor. Include your firm's logo, a personalized subject line (e.g., *"{{contact.first_name}}, your feedback means the world to us"*), and a clear CTA button linking directly to Google.

If you want to dive deeper into configuring automated emails in GoHighLevel, check out the [email marketing automation guide](/blog/gohighlevel-email-marketing-automation).

## 5. Monitor Results and Set Up Alerts for Negative Reviews

Setting up the automatic request is only half the job. The other half is monitoring incoming reviews and responding quickly — especially to negative ones.

**How to monitor in GoHighLevel:**

1. Go to **Reputation → Reviews**. You'll see all incoming reviews in chronological order, with a filter by star rating.
2. To set up email alerts when a new review comes in, go to **Reputation → Settings → Notifications** and enable **Email Notifications for New Reviews**.
3. For 1- or 2-star reviews, GoHighLevel can notify a specific team member — set the email address of whoever should be alerted immediately.

**Responding to reviews with AI:**

In the *Reviews* tab, click on any review and select **Generate AI Response**. GoHighLevel uses artificial intelligence to suggest a response that matches the tone of the review. You review it, edit if needed, and publish — all without leaving the platform.

> **Important tip for law firms:** Never disclose case details or confirm that someone is a client in public responses — this could violate attorney-client confidentiality obligations. Keep responses professional and general, and invite the person to continue the conversation privately.

**Track these metrics weekly:**

- **Send rate:** How many requests were triggered
- **Conversion rate:** How many turned into actual reviews (healthy benchmark: 15–25%)
- **Average rating:** Should trend upward as new reviews come in
- **Response time:** Ideally under 24 hours for any review

All of this data is available in the **Reputation Dashboard** within GoHighLevel, accessible from your sub-account's main menu.
