## 02. Access the Calendars Section in GoHighLevel

First, let's find the feature inside the platform.

**What you'll see on screen:**
After logging into your GoHighLevel account, you'll see the left-side navigation menu with icons. Look for the calendar icon — typically represented by a grid symbol with days of the week.

**Step by step:**

1. In the left-side navigation menu, click on **"Calendars"**
2. You'll be taken to the main Calendars screen, where you'll see a blue **"+ Create Calendar"** button in the top-right corner
3. Click that button to open the creation wizard

**Pro tip:** If your account has multiple users (e.g., several attorneys or consultants), each person can have their own calendar. You can also create calendars by service type — for example, "Initial Consultation" and "Follow-Up Meeting."

## 03. Configure Your Calendar's Basic Information

After clicking "+ Create Calendar," a configuration window will open. This is where you define your calendar's identity.

**What you'll see on screen:**
A form with text fields, color selectors, and calendar type options. You'll see three type options: **Event Calendar**, **Service Calendar**, and **Round Robin Calendar**.

**Choose the right type:**

| Type | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Event Calendar | Webinars, workshops, group sessions — one date, multiple attendees |
| Service Calendar | Individual consultations, assessments, 1:1 meetings |
| Round Robin Calendar | Teams with multiple staff members — distributes bookings automatically |

For most service-based businesses, the **Service Calendar** is the right choice.

**Fill in the fields:**

- **Calendar name:** Use something descriptive. Ex: "Free 30-Minute Consultation" or "Home Cleaning Assessment"
- **Description:** Briefly explain what happens during this time slot (the client sees this information)
- **Duration:** Select the default meeting length (15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes — or a custom duration)
- **Color:** Choose a color to visually identify this calendar

**Pro tip:** Use the calendar name as a mini value proposition. "Free 30-Minute Consultation" converts far better than just "Meeting."

## 04. Set Your Availability and Booking Rules

This is the most critical step in the setup. Here you tell the system when you (or your team) are available to accept bookings.

**What you'll see on screen:**
A weekly grid with days of the week and start/end time fields for each day. Below that, options for "buffer" time and the booking window.

**Configure your availability:**

1. Enable the days you're available (click the toggle next to each day)
2. For each active day, set the start and end time (e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM)
3. If you have breaks (lunch, for example), click **"+ Add Break"** and define the time block

**Configure time buffers:**

- **Before buffer:** Preparation time before each appointment (we recommend 10–15 minutes)
- **After buffer:** Time for notes and follow-up after each meeting (we recommend 10 minutes)

**Configure the booking window:**

- **Minimum notice:** How many hours in advance a client must book. We recommend at least 2–4 hours to avoid last-minute bookings you won't see in time.
- **Maximum window:** How many days ahead a client can book. 30 to 60 days is the standard for most businesses.

**Pro tip:** Enable **"Check for Conflicts with Google Calendar"** or **"Outlook"** if you use those services. GHL syncs in real time and automatically blocks time slots already taken on your personal calendar.

## 05. Set Up Automatic Confirmations and Reminders

This is where GoHighLevel far outperforms tools like Calendly. You can set up a complete automated communication sequence — all without leaving the platform.

**What you'll see on screen:**
A tab called **"Notifications"** or **"Confirmations"** inside the calendar settings, with fields for confirmation, reminder, and cancellation emails and SMS messages.

**Configure your messages:**

### Immediate Confirmation (sent right after booking)

- **Type:** Email + SMS
- **Email subject:** "Your appointment is confirmed — {{appointment.title}}"
- **Body:** Confirm the date, time, format (in-person or virtual), and what the client should prepare
- **SMS:** "Hi {{contact.first_name}}! Your consultation is confirmed for {{appointment.start_time}}. See you then!"

### Reminder (sent 24 hours before)

- **SMS:** "Reminder: your consultation is tomorrow at {{appointment.start_time}}. Reply YES to confirm or NO to cancel."

### Final Reminder (sent 1 hour before)

- **SMS:** "Your meeting starts in 1 hour. Here's your link: {{appointment.meeting_link}}"

**Pro tip:** Always include the virtual meeting link (Zoom, Google Meet) directly in the 1-hour reminder. This dramatically reduces late arrivals and no-shows.

## 06. Embed the Calendar on Your Website or Funnel

Once your calendar is configured, you need to make the link or widget available so clients can actually book.

**What you'll see on screen:**
Inside the calendar settings, a tab called **"Link & Embed"** with a direct URL and an HTML embed code.

**Option 1 — Direct link:**
Copy the public calendar link (format: `https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/booking/YOURSLUG`) and share it in:
- Your email signature
- Your Instagram or social media bio
- Automated SMS and WhatsApp messages
- Your Google Business Profile

**Option 2 — Embedded widget on your website:**
Copy the HTML code provided by GHL and paste it into the page on your website where you want to display the calendar. Inside GoHighLevel's own page builder, there's a **"Calendar Widget"** element — simply drag and drop it directly into your funnel or landing page.

**Option 3 — Calendar inside a conversion funnel:**

## Common Mistakes When Setting Up GoHighLevel Calendar

The Vida Digital Solutions team has implemented the calendar module for dozens of businesses. These are the most frequent mistakes we see:

**1. Not syncing with your personal calendar**
Without syncing to Google Calendar or Outlook, you may receive bookings during times you already have other commitments. Enable two-way sync in the integration settings.

**2. Forgetting time buffers**
Without buffers, meetings run back-to-back with no breathing room. Always configure at least 10 minutes of buffer time after each appointment.

**3. Not setting minimum notice time**
Without this setting, a client could book an appointment 15 minutes from now and you won't see the notification in time.

**4. Using generic confirmation messages**
Personalize your confirmation and reminder texts with the client's name and appointment details. A generic SMS looks like spam; a personalized one builds trust.

**5. Not testing the full flow**
Before going live, book a test appointment using the public link. Verify that notifications are delivered correctly, that calendar sync is working, and that cancellation and rescheduling options are enabled.
