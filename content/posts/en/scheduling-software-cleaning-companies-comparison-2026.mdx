## Jobber: The Field Service Classic

**Jobber** is one of the most well-known tools among residential service businesses in the US. It's built specifically for businesses like cleaning, landscaping, plumbing, and electrical.

### Jobber Pros

- Simple, intuitive interface — easy to learn in less than a week
- Robust mobile app to manage jobs in the field
- Visual drag-and-drop scheduling
- Integrated invoicing and billing
- Automatic appointment reminders via SMS and email
- QuickBooks integration

### Jobber Cons

- No sales pipeline or lead CRM
- Limited marketing automations — requires external tools like Mailchimp
- No customizable online booking page optimized for conversion
- Does not manage Google reviews
- More complete plans reach $169/month and still lack marketing features
- English support only

**Ideal profile:** A cleaning company with a solid existing client base that wants to organize operations, without a focus on digital marketing growth.

## Housecall Pro: More Complete, Still Has Gaps

**Housecall Pro** competes directly with Jobber but offers a few extra features that are useful for businesses looking to scale.

### Housecall Pro Pros

- Online booking portal for clients
- Integrated payment processing (including credit cards)
- Performance reports and dashboard
- Built-in referral program
- In-platform chat communication with clients
- Plans starting at $79/month

### Housecall Pro Cons

- Basic CRM — does not track communication history in an advanced way
- No automated inactive client reactivation
- No lead capture funnel
- Integrations with marketing tools are paid separately
- Limited customization for bilingual businesses (Spanish/English)
- English support only

**Ideal profile:** A growing cleaning company that needs online scheduling and payments, but will still use separate tools for marketing.
