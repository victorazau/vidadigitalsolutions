## How Bilingual Automation Solves This in Practice

Bilingual automation for law firms is a system that detects a lead's language — via form selection, SMS reply, or a designated field — and triggers all subsequent communication **in that language, automatically**, without any human intervention.

In GoHighLevel, here's how it works:

### Step 1 — Smart Capture
Your website form includes one simple field: "Preferred language / Idioma preferido." Based on the selection, the lead enters a dedicated pipeline: English, Portuguese, or Spanish.

### Step 2 — Immediate Response (under 5 minutes)
An automated SMS goes out in the client's chosen language:
- 🇧🇷 *"Olá! Recebemos seu contato. Um de nossos advogados vai te ligar em breve. Enquanto isso, confirme aqui seu horário preferido para a consulta: [link]"*
- 🇲🇽 *"¡Hola! Recibimos tu mensaje. Un abogado te llamará pronto. Confirma tu horario preferido aquí: [link]"*
- 🇺🇸 *"Hi! We received your message. One of our attorneys will call you shortly. In the meantime, confirm your preferred consultation time here: [link]"*

### Step 3 — Automated Nurture Sequence
If the lead doesn't schedule within the first 24 hours, a bilingual email and SMS sequence fires over the next 7 days — explaining the process, addressing common questions, and building credibility for your firm. All in the client's language.

### Step 4 — Automated Intake
Once the client schedules, they receive a pre-consultation intake form in their language. Your attorney gets the completed information organized before the meeting — saving 30 to 45 minutes per consultation.

### Step 5 — Reminders and Confirmation
Automated reminders go out 24 hours and 2 hours before the consultation, with a link to cancel or reschedule. This cuts no-shows by up to 60%.
