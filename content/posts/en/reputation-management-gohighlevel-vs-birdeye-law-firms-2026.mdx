When a potential client searches "immigration lawyer near me" or "family lawyer in Houston" on Google — or asks that same question to ChatGPT or Perplexity — what shows up first isn't necessarily the oldest or most expensive firm. It's the firm with the most positive, recent reviews.

Birdeye is one of the most respected tools on the market when it comes to **exclusively** managing reviews and satisfaction surveys (NPS). It has a well-developed interface built specifically for that purpose, along with integrations across 200+ review platforms.

- **Integration overhead** — connecting Birdeye to your existing CRM (Clio, MyCase, etc.) requires technical setup or additional integration fees
