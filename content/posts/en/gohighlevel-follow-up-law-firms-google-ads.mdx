## Step 1 — Set Up Your Legal Pipeline in GoHighLevel

Before building any automation, you need a structured pipeline that reflects the actual stages of your intake process.

**What you'll see on screen:** In the left-hand sidebar of GoHighLevel, click **Opportunities**, then **Pipelines**. Click the blue **+ Add Pipeline** button in the upper right corner.

Create a pipeline called **"Legal Intake"** with the following stages:

| Stage | What It Means |
|---|---|
| New Lead | Form submitted, no contact made yet |
| Contact Attempted | SMS/email sent, awaiting response |
| Consultation Scheduled | Lead confirmed a time slot |
| Consultation Completed | Meeting took place |
| Proposal Sent | Contract under negotiation |
| Signed Client | Contract closed |
| Disqualified | Lead not qualified or not interested |

**Pro tip:** Don't overcomplicate your pipeline. A 10-stage pipeline looks thorough on paper, but in practice nobody keeps it updated. Seven stages is the recommended maximum for small to mid-sized firms.

Once you've created the pipeline, click **Save** and it will appear in your Opportunities dashboard.

## Step 2 — Connect Your Google Ads Form to GoHighLevel

For the automation to work, leads need to enter GoHighLevel the moment they submit a form. There are two main ways to do this:

**Option A — GoHighLevel Native Form:**
Go to **Sites → Forms → + New Form**. Create a form with the following fields: Name, Phone, Email, and Case Type. Copy the generated link and use it as the destination URL in your Google Ads campaign. When someone submits it, the contact is created automatically.

**Option B — Webhook for an External Form:**
If you already have a website with its own form (WordPress, for example), go to **Settings → Integrations → Webhooks** and copy the endpoint URL. Configure your form to send data to that endpoint via POST. It's more technical, but works seamlessly.

**What you'll see on screen when your first lead comes in:** Under the **Contacts** tab, a new contact will appear with the submitted information and the tag **"Google Ads Lead"** automatically applied — as long as you configure that tag in the form settings.

## Step 3 — Build the Immediate Follow-Up Workflow

This is the engine of the entire system. The immediate follow-up workflow is what makes your firm respond in under 60 seconds, even at 11 PM.

**What you'll see on screen:** Go to **Automation → Workflows → + New Workflow**. Select the **Start from Scratch** option.

**Trigger:** Click **Add Trigger** and select **Contact Created**. Under **Filters**, add the condition **Tag contains "Google Ads Lead"**. This ensures the workflow only fires for leads coming from your ads.

**Action sequence — set it up like this:**

**Action 1 — Immediate SMS (0-minute delay):**
Add **Send SMS**. Use a message like:
> *"Hi {{contact.first_name}}! We received your inquiry with [Firm Name]. One of our attorneys will be in touch shortly. To help us assist you faster — what is your most pressing concern right now?"*

Use the dynamic variables (double curly braces) available in the GoHighLevel editor — they automatically populate the contact's name.

**Action 2 — Confirmation email (1-minute delay):**
Add a **Wait Step** of 1 minute, then **Send Email**. The email should confirm receipt, briefly introduce the firm, and include a link to schedule a consultation online (you'll set this up in Step 4).

**Action 3 — Create opportunity in pipeline (immediate):**
Add **Create Opportunity** and link it to the **"Legal Intake"** pipeline at the **"Contact Attempted"** stage.

**Action 4 — Notify your internal team (immediate):**
Add an **Internal Notification** to send an email or SMS to the responsible attorney with the lead's details. That way, when they open their laptop in the morning, they already know who's waiting.

**Action 5 — Secondary follow-up (24-hour delay):**
If the lead hasn't responded, add a second SMS the next day:
> *"{{contact.first_name}}, are you still looking for help with your case? We have availability this week. [scheduling link]"*

**Pro tip:** Add an **If/Else** condition before the secondary follow-up to check whether the lead has already replied (tag **"Responded"** is present). If yes, the workflow stops. If no, it sends the SMS. This prevents unnecessary messages to leads who've already engaged.

Click **Save** and activate the workflow using the toggle in the upper right corner.

## Step 4 — Set Up Your Automatic Consultation Calendar

The scheduling link you'll include in your SMS and email sequences needs to be configured before you activate the workflow.

**What you'll see on screen:** Go to **Calendars → + New Calendar**. Choose **Round Robin** if you have multiple attorneys handling intake, or **Personal** if it's just you.

**Essential settings:**

- **Calendar name:** Initial Consultation — [Firm Name]
- **Duration:** 30 minutes (initial consultations should be focused and efficient)
- **Buffer after meeting:** 10 minutes (time for notes and follow-up)
- **Available hours:** Only list times when someone will actually be there — don't offer Sunday slots if no one's working
- **Intake form:** Add fields like Case Type, Citizenship Status, and Urgency Level. This information arrives with the contact before the consultation, letting the attorney prepare in advance

**Automatic confirmations:** Still inside the calendar settings, enable **Appointment Confirmations**. GoHighLevel will automatically send a confirmation email and SMS reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the consultation.

Copy the generated calendar link and paste it into your follow-up messages in the Step 3 workflows.

## Step 5 — Enable Performance Reporting and Optimize

There's no point configuring all of this without knowing what's actually working. GoHighLevel has built-in reports that show you exactly where leads are converting — or dropping off.

**What you'll see on screen:** Go to **Reporting → Appointments** to see your booking rate. Go to **Opportunities → Pipeline** and click **View Report** to see how quickly leads are moving between stages.

**Metrics to review every week:**

| Metric | Recommended Target |
|---|---|
| Response time (lead → SMS) | Under 2 minutes |
| Initial SMS reply rate | Above 30% |
| Consultation booking rate | Above 25% of leads |
| Show-up rate | Above 70% |
| Post-consultation close rate | Above 40% |

**Optimization tip:** If your booking rate is low, the issue is usually the SMS copy or a calendar with too few available time slots. Test message variations using the **A/B Testing** feature in your workflows.

If your show-up rate is low, add a third SMS reminder 2 hours before the consultation — firms that do this report a 20–30% increase in attendance.
