## Step 1 — Connect Your Google Business Profile to GoHighLevel

The first step is linking your Google Business Profile to GoHighLevel so the platform can monitor your reviews and generate the correct review link.

**How to do it:**

1. In the GoHighLevel left sidebar, click on **Reputation** (star icon)
2. Select the **Settings** tab
3. Click **Connect Google Account**
4. A pop-up window will appear asking you to log in to your Google account
5. After logging in, select your business's **Google Business Profile** from the list
6. Click **Allow** to authorize the integration

**What you'll see on screen:** A green confirmation panel showing "Google Business Profile Connected" with your business name, address, and current rating. From this point on, all your Google reviews appear in real time inside GoHighLevel.

> **Pro tip:** If you manage multiple locations or clients (like an agency), you can connect a different Google Business Profile to each GoHighLevel sub-account.

## Step 2 — Set Up Your Custom Review Link

The review link is the exact URL you'll send to customers. When they click it, they land directly on your Google review screen — no searching required.

**How to do it:**

1. Still in **Reputation > Settings**, find the **Review Link** section
2. GoHighLevel automatically generates your Google Business Profile review link
3. Copy the link and **test it by clicking** — you should land directly on your business's Google review screen
4. If you'd like to shorten the link (recommended for SMS), use the native shortener by clicking **Shorten URL**

**What you'll see on screen:** A box with the full link and a "Copy" button beside it. Below, a preview showing how the link will appear to the recipient.

> **Why this matters:** Long links in SMS messages look unprofessional and hurt click-through rates. A short link like `reviews.yourbusiness.com/google` converts significantly better.

## Step 3 — Build the Automatic Review Request Workflow

This is where the magic happens. You'll create an automation that fires automatically after a job is completed — no need to remember to message anyone.

**How to do it:**

1. In the sidebar, click **Automation > Workflows**
2. Click **+ Create Workflow** and select **Start from Scratch**
3. Give it a clear name: "Review Request — Post-Service"
4. Click **Add Trigger** and select the event that best fits your business:
   - **Appointment Status Changed** → Status: Completed (for appointment-based businesses)
   - **Pipeline Stage Changed** → select the "Service Completed" stage (if you use a pipeline)
   - **Tag Added** → Tag: "review-request" (to trigger manually whenever you choose)
5. Click **Save Trigger**

**Adding the workflow actions:**

**Action 1 — Wait:**
- Click **+** to add an action
- Select **Wait**
- Set it to **1 hour** after the trigger fires (ideal timing: the customer's experience is still fresh)

**Action 2 — Send SMS:**
- Add the **Send SMS** action
- In the text field, write:
  > "Hi {{contact.first_name}}! It was a pleasure serving you today. If you're happy with our work, a quick Google review would mean the world to us: {{review_link}}. Thank you! 🌟"
- Replace `{{review_link}}` with the link generated in Step 2

**Action 3 — Wait 24 Hours (Follow-up):**
- Add another **Wait** of 24 hours

**Action 4 — If/Else Condition:**
- Add an **If/Else** action
- Condition: **Review Received = Yes** (GoHighLevel detects when a new review is posted)
- **YES Branch:** End the workflow (don't bother customers who already reviewed)
- **NO Branch:** Continue to the next action

**Action 5 — Send Follow-up Email:**
- Add **Send Email**
- Subject: "Your feedback means everything to us 🙏"
- Body: A short email thanking them for their business and repeating the review request with the link

6. Click **Save**, then activate the workflow by toggling it to **Published**

**What you'll see on screen:** A visual flow with blocks connected by arrows, showing the sequence: Trigger → Wait 1h → SMS → Wait 24h → If/Else → Email. A green color indicates the workflow is live.

## Step 4 — Monitor and Respond to Reviews From the Dashboard

With the system running, you need to track results and respond to reviews — both positive and negative. Responding to reviews is a confirmed Google ranking factor.

**How to do it:**

1. Go to **Reputation > Overview** in the sidebar
2. You'll see a dashboard showing:
   - **Current average rating** (e.g., ⭐ 4.7)
   - **Total reviews** by platform (Google, Facebook)
   - **Trend chart** over time
   - **Recent reviews feed** with date, rating, and review text
3. To respond to a review, click the **Reply** button next to it
4. Type your response directly in the text field and click **Post Reply**

**What you'll see on screen:** A card for each review showing the customer's profile photo, star rating, review text, date, and a blue "Reply" button. Unanswered reviews display a yellow alert icon.

> **Pro tip:** Create response templates to save time. In GoHighLevel, you can save standard replies under **Settings > Response Templates** and insert them with a single click.

## Step 5 — Send Manual Review Requests to Past Customers

In addition to the automated workflow, GoHighLevel lets you send review requests manually — perfect for re-engaging past customers who never left a review.

**How to do it:**

1. Go to **Reputation > Get Reviews**
2. Click **Send Review Request**
3. You can send to an individual contact or to a list/tag
4. Select your channel: SMS or Email
5. Edit the message if you'd like, then click **Send**

**Recommended strategy:** Create a tag called "no-review-yet" and add all active customers who haven't reviewed you. Send a one-time campaign to that list with a personalized message. Many businesses generate 10 to 20 new reviews from this single action alone.
